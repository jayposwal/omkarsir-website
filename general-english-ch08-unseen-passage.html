11. यह टॉपिक RAS Mains में क्यों महत्वपूर्ण है? — पूरा Exam Pattern

RPSC RAS Mains परीक्षा योजना (Paper-IV, General English) के Part B — 'Comprehension, Translation & Précis Writing' खंड में कुल 30 अंक हैं, और इसका एक बड़ा हिस्सा सीधे Unseen Passage पर आधारित है। यह टॉपिक इसलिए भी महत्वपूर्ण है क्योंकि इसमें रटने की ज़रूरत नहीं — सिर्फ सही तकनीक और अभ्यास से पूरे अंक लाए जा सकते हैं।

⚠️ मूल सिद्धांत (Core Principle): Comprehension में उत्तर कहीं बाहर से नहीं आता — वह हमेशा passage के अंदर ही मौजूद होता है। आपका काम सिर्फ passage में से सही जानकारी ढूंढना और उसे अपने शब्दों में, साफ़ व संक्षिप्त वाक्य में लिखना है। अपनी तरफ़ से कोई नई जानकारी या राय मत जोड़िए जब तक प्रश्न खुद राय (opinion) न मांगे।

22. Comprehension हल करने की तकनीक — Step by Step

Step 1: पहला वाचन (Skim Reading) — 2-3 मिनट

पूरे passage को एक बार तेज़ी से पढ़ें — हर शब्द समझने की कोशिश मत करें। इस पहले वाचन का मकसद सिर्फ यह समझना है कि passage किस विषय (topic) के बारे में है, लेखक का मुख्य भाव (central idea) क्या है, और टोन (गंभीर/हास्यपूर्ण/आलोचनात्मक) कैसा है।

Step 2: दूसरा वाचन (Detailed Reading) — 4-5 मिनट

अब प्रश्न पढ़ें (यदि पहले से दिए हों), फिर passage को धीरे-धीरे दोबारा पढ़ें। जो lines किसी प्रश्न से जुड़ी लगें, उन्हें मन में या हल्के से underline करें। कठिन शब्दों के आसपास के वाक्य (context) पर ध्यान दें — वहीं से अर्थ का अंदाज़ा लगता है।

Step 3: उत्तर लिखना — 12-15 मिनट

  • ➤ हर उत्तर पूरे, सही व्याकरण वाले वाक्य में लिखें — सिर्फ एक शब्द या अधूरा वाक्यांश न लिखें।
  • ➤ जहाँ तक संभव हो अपने शब्दों (own words) में लिखें — पूरा वाक्य ज्यों-का-त्यों passage से मत उठाइए, हालांकि passage के मुख्य keyword का प्रयोग करना ठीक है।
  • ➤ उत्तर संक्षिप्त (concise) रखें — 1-2 वाक्य ही काफ़ी हैं, जब तक प्रश्न में विस्तार से समझाने को न कहा जाए।
  • ➤ शब्दार्थ (meaning of a word) वाले प्रश्न में सीधा शब्दकोश-अर्थ न रटें — passage के context में वह शब्द जिस भाव से प्रयोग हुआ है, वही भाव लिखें।

33. प्रश्नों के प्रकार — पहचान एवं उत्तर लिखने का तरीका

प्रश्न का प्रकारपहचान कैसे करेंउत्तर लिखने का तरीका
Factual / Direct (तथ्यात्मक)प्रश्न में 'What/Who/When/Where/How many' जैसे सीधे शब्द होते हैं; उत्तर passage की किसी एक line में सीधा मिल जाता है।उस line को अपने शब्दों में दोबारा लिखें — तथ्य बिल्कुल न बदलें।
Inferential (अनुमान-आधारित)प्रश्न में 'Why do you think', 'What can be inferred' जैसे शब्द होते हैं; उत्तर सीधा नहीं लिखा होता, passage की कई lines जोड़कर निकालना पड़ता है।पूरे paragraph के भाव को जोड़कर एक तार्किक (logical) निष्कर्ष लिखें — पर passage की सीमा से बाहर मत जाइए।
Vocabulary-in-Context (शब्दार्थ)प्रश्न पूछता है 'What is the meaning of the word ___ as used in the passage?'शब्दकोश का सामान्य अर्थ नहीं — passage के उस विशेष वाक्य में शब्द जिस भाव से आया है, वही भाव लिखें।
Title / Theme (शीर्षक/मूल भाव)प्रश्न पूछता है 'Suggest a suitable title' या 'What is the central idea?'3-5 शब्दों का संक्षिप्त, अर्थपूर्ण शीर्षक दें — पूरा वाक्य न बनाएं, कोई Article (a/an/the) भी सामान्यतः न लगाएं।
Opinion / Agree-Disagree (राय)प्रश्न सीधे पूछता है 'Do you agree...? Why?'यहीं एकमात्र जगह है जहाँ अपनी राय दी जा सकती है — पर उसे भी passage के तर्कों (arguments) से जोड़कर justify करें।

नीचे 11 गद्यांश दिए गए हैं — बिल्कुल असली RAS Mains पैटर्न के अनुसार, हर एक के साथ 5 प्रश्न व मॉडल उत्तर, और अंत में उसी गद्यांश की Précis की एक झलक (Bonus)। पहले खुद गद्यांश पढ़कर प्रश्नों के उत्तर लिखने की कोशिश करें, फिर नीचे दिए मॉडल उत्तर से मिलाएं।

गद्यांश 1

📖 Passage 1 — ध्यान से पढ़ें (लगभग 254 शब्द)

In everyday speech, we talk of having a good memory, of having a poor memory, of having a better memory for faces than for names, of having a memory that is failing, and so on. Such talk suggests that memory is an object, a thing which we possess in the same way as we possess a head or a big toe. Yet it is true, although alarming, to say that there is no such thing as memory. A big toe can be seen and touched, but not so memory. Should an acquaintance boast of having an excellent memory, we cannot confirm his claim in the same way as if he boasted the possession of a well- stocked. library. We cannot ask him to show us his memory. What we could do, how-ever, is to let him read the page of a book, then have him close the book and try to recite what he has just read. If he reels off the page verbatim, we conclude that his claim is, at the least, not without foundation. But we have not observed anything which could be called a memory. We have given him an opportunity to learn something and then to demonstrate how well he remembers it. We have not examined anything which he has, but have watched him doing something, namely, repeating or trying to repeat something he has read. In short, we have concerned ourselves, not with an object but with an activity, not with his memory but with his activity of remembering.

📝 प्रश्न 1.1: What is the popular belief about memory?
✅ उत्तर: The popular belief about memory is that it is a thing we possess, like a body part, which can be good, poor, or failing.
📝 प्रश्न 1.2: What example does the writer give to support the statement that "there is no such thing as memory'?
✅ उत्तर: The writer gives the example of a person claiming to have a great memory. We cannot see or touch memory, but we can test it by making the person read a page and then recall it.
📝 प्रश्न 1.3: How does the writer distinguish between the terms 'memory' and 'remembering?
✅ उत्तर: The writer explains that memory is not an object but an activity. Remembering is the action of recalling things, while memory is wrongly thought of as a physical possession.
📝 प्रश्न 1.4: What is the meaning of the word "Verbatim”?
✅ उत्तर: "Verbatim" means word-for-word, or exactly as it was written or spoken.
📝 प्रश्न 1.5: Do you agree with the author's distinction between '' memory and remembering’? Why?
✅ उत्तर: Yes, the distinction makes sense. Memory is not something we own but an ability to recall information. Remembering is the process of using that ability.
🔖 बोनस झलक — Précis: "Memory as an Activity, Not a Thing"

People often speak of memory as if it were a thing we possess, like a body part. However, memory cannot be seen or touched. We cannot directly prove someone has a good memory by looking at it. Instead, we test it by observing how well a person remembers something, such as repeating a passage after reading it. This shows that memory is not an object but an activity of remembering.
(Précis लिखने की पूरी तकनीक अगले अध्याय — Précis Writing — में सीखेंगे)

गद्यांश 2

📖 Passage 2 — ध्यान से पढ़ें (लगभग 222 शब्द)

The thoughtless, the ignorant and the indolent seeing only the apparent effect of things and not the things themselves talk of luck of fortunes and chance. Seeing a man grow rich they say "How lucky he is!" Observing another becomes intellectual they exclaim, "How highly favoured he is!" And noting the saintly character and wide influence of someone else they remark, "How chance aids him at every turn!" They do not see the trials and failures and struggles which these men have voluntarily encountered in order to gain their experience, have no knowledge of the sacrifices they have made, of the undaunted efforts they have put forth, of the faith they have exercised that they might overcome the apparently insurmountable and realise the vision of their heart. They do not know the darkness and the heartaches; they only see the light and joy; do not see the long and arduous journey but only behold the pleasant goal and call it "good fortune; do not understand the process but only perceive the results and call it "chance". In all human affairs there are efforts and there are results and the strength of effort is the measure of the result. Chance is not. Gifts, powers, material, intellectual and spiritual possessions are the fruits of effort - they are thoughts completed, objects accomplished, visions realized.

📝 प्रश्न 2.1: Which people, seeing the apparent effect of things, talk of luck, fortune and chance?
✅ उत्तर: The thoughtless, the ignorant, and the indolent, who see only the apparent effect of things and not the things themselves, talk of luck, fortune, and chance.
📝 प्रश्न 2.2: When do such people consider luck and chance to favour an individual?
✅ उत्तर: Such people consider luck and chance to favour an individual when they see someone growing rich, becoming intellectual, or gaining a saintly character and wide influence.
📝 प्रश्न 2.3: What is needed to realize the vision of the heart?
✅ उत्तर: To realize the vision of the heart, one needs to face trials, failures, and struggles voluntarily, make sacrifices, put forth undaunted efforts, and exercise faith to overcome difficulties.
📝 प्रश्न 2.4: What is the connection between efforts and results as stated in the passage?
✅ उत्तर: The passage states that in all human affairs, efforts lead to results, and the strength of effort determines the measure of the result. It emphasizes that achievements are not due to chance but are the fruits of dedicated effort.
📝 प्रश्न 2.5: Find a word in the passage that has the same meaning as the following:
✅ उत्तर: Not intimidated or discouraged by difficulty, danger or disappointed. The word from the passage that means "not intimidated or discouraged by difficulty, danger, or disappointment" is "undaunted".
🔖 बोनस झलक — Précis: "Success the Result of Effort, Not Luck"

People often think success comes from luck or chance because they only see the results, not the effort behind them. When someone becomes rich, intelligent, or respected, they call it good fortune. However, they do not see the hard work, struggles, sacrifices, and determination that led to that success. In reality, success is the result of continuous effort, not chance. All achievements, whether material or intellectual, are gained through hard work and strong effort.
(Précis लिखने की पूरी तकनीक अगले अध्याय — Précis Writing — में सीखेंगे)

गद्यांश 3

📖 Passage 3 — ध्यान से पढ़ें (लगभग 259 शब्द)

Conversation is indeed the most easily teachable of all arts. All you need to do in order to become a good conversationalist is to find a subject that interests you and your listeners. There are, for example, numberless hobbies to talk about. But the important thing is that you must talk about the other fellow's hobby rather than your own. Therein lies the secret of your popularity. Talk to your friends about things that interest them and you will make your reputation for good fellowship, charming wit and a brilliant mind. There is nothing that pleases people more than your interest in their interest. It is as important to know what subject to avoid as what subjects to select for good conversation. If you don't want to be set down as a wet blanket or a bore, be careful to avoid unpleasant topics. Avoid talking about yourself, unless you are asked to do so. People are interested in their problems not in yours. Sickness and death bore everybody. The only one who willingly listens to such talk is a doctor, but he gets paid for it. To be a good conversationalist you must know not only what to say but how to say it. Be civil and modest. Don't ever emphasize your own happiness. Be mentally quick and witty, but don't hurt others with your wit. Finally try to avoid mannerisms in your conversation. Don't bite your lips, or click your tongue, or roll your eyes, or use hands excessively as you speak. Such gestures detract from the conversation.

📝 प्रश्न 3.1: What subjects of conversation make one popular ?
✅ उत्तर: Subjects that interest the listeners, especially their hobbies, make one popular in conversation. Talking about others’ interests rather than one's own helps in building a good reputation.
📝 प्रश्न 3.2: What topics must one avoid in a good conversation?
✅ उत्तर: One must avoid unpleasant topics such as sickness, death, and personal problems. It is also advised not to talk excessively about oneself unless asked.
📝 प्रश्न 3.3: What are the two things a good conversationalist must know ?
✅ उत्तर: A good conversationalist must know what to say and how to say it.
📝 प्रश्न 3.4: How should a good conversationalist treat others?
✅ उत्तर: A good conversationalist should be civil, modest, mentally quick, and witty. They should avoid hurting others with their wit and should not display distracting mannerisms.
📝 प्रश्न 3.5: Find the word/words in the passage that have the same meaning as the following: A person who discourages enjoyment.
✅ उत्तर: The word that means "a person who discourages enjoyment" is "wet blanket".
🔖 बोनस झलक — Précis: "Art of Good Conversation"

Conversation is easy to learn if we talk about topics that interest others, not just ourselves. Showing interest in others makes us likable and popular. We should avoid unpleasant topics like personal problems, sickness, or death, as they bore people. A good conversationalist is polite, modest, and careful in speech. One should be witty but not hurt others. Proper behaviour and avoiding odd gestures also help in making conversation pleasant.
(Précis लिखने की पूरी तकनीक अगले अध्याय — Précis Writing — में सीखेंगे)

गद्यांश 4

📖 Passage 4 — ध्यान से पढ़ें (लगभग 260 शब्द)

Nature is our mother and if it is to be commanded, it must be obeyed. But man's ignorance of this is the main source of his unhappiness. Since the beginning, man has been using the priceless gifts of nature without restraint. He has rather taken them for granted. Seldom did he think that the supplies of air, water and food and the industrial raw materials that he extracted from the bowels of the earth might disappear one day. Nature has been suffering because of thoughtless human intervention in its recycling process for hundreds of years. They say it truly that we have not inherited this earth from our ancestors, but have borrowed it from our children. We are obliged to preserve Nature for the benefit of future generations. But what has man been doing to mother Nature? The forests have become concrete jungles. The marvellous creatures and plants dependent on the rich Nature are finding it difficult to survive in the face of man's cruelty. The ozone layer protecting the earth's atmosphere is gradually getting depleted. So desperate are we to enjoy our today that tomorrow does not seem all that important. It is now that man has started realizing that he has to work with Nature and cannot allow its destruction. Man has begun to understand that Nature and its resources are vast but limited. Nature's gifts are to be conserved. Let's all make a joint venture to make nature as pure as before and try to maintain the immortal relationship of mother and son between Nature and man.

📝 प्रश्न 4.1: What is causing unhappiness to man?
✅ उत्तर: Man’s unhappiness is caused by his ignorance of nature’s importance and his reckless exploitation of natural resources, leading to environmental destruction and resource depletion.
📝 प्रश्न 4.2: Why is it important to be careful in our use of the resources of the earth?
✅ उत्तर: Earth’s resources are limited and essential for survival. If overused, they may disappear, harming future generations and disrupting ecological balance.
📝 प्रश्न 4.3: What do we owe to our future generations ?
✅ उत्तर: We must preserve and conserve nature so that future generations can enjoy clean air, water, forests, and a healthy environment.
📝 प्रश्न 4.4: What recent change has taken place in man's attitude to Nature ?
✅ उत्तर: Man has realized that nature and its resources are not limitless and must be protected for survival.
📝 प्रश्न 4.5: Pick out words from the text that mean
✅ उत्तर: (a) to limit. Control → Commanded (b) slowly, over a long period of time. → Gradually
🔖 बोनस झलक — Précis: "Need to Protect Nature"

Nature is like our mother, and we must respect and protect it. Humans have used natural resources carelessly without thinking about the future. This has harmed the environment, causing problems like deforestation, loss of wildlife, and depletion of the ozone layer. We must remember that we have borrowed the Earth from future generations. Now, people are beginning to realize the importance of conserving nature. We should work together to protect and preserve it for a better future.

Sleep is essential for a person's health and well-being. Yet millions of people do not get enough sleep and many suffer from lack of sleep. Most of those with these problems go undiagnosed and untreated. Everyone's individual sleep needs vary. In general, most healthy adults are built for 16 hours of wakefulness and need an average of eight hours of sleep a night. However, some individuals are able to function without sleepiness or drowsiness after as little as six hours of sleep. Others can't perform at their peak unless they've slept ten hours. And contrary to common myth, the need for sleep doesn't decline with age but the ability to sleep for six to eight hours at one time may be reduced. It is a good idea to talk to a physician, or mental health provider about any sleeping problem that recurs or persists for longer than a few weeks.

Rajasthan, one of the driest states of India, has for centuries taught the world a valuable lesson in water conservation. In recent decades, as groundwater levels fell due to over-extraction and changing rainfall patterns, many villages returned to reviving these traditional structures instead of relying solely on borewells. Social organisations, working alongside local communities, have restored hundreds of johads and tankas, turning several dry villages green again and reversing large-scale migration in some regions. The revival of these age-old techniques proves an important truth: sustainable solutions to modern environmental problems often already exist in traditional wisdom; they only need to be recognised, respected, and restored rather than replaced entirely by imported technology.

Of the many questions that I have been asked by interviewers over the last ten years or so, two have recurred more frequently than others...... 'How did you come into films?' and 'Why did you come into films?' This has generally been asked in the knowledge that I had started my career in advertising as a graphic designer..... After all, both films and advertising deal with consumable commodities and in both you have the spectacle of the conscientious artist striving to express himself in aesthetic terms; while the sponsor, hovering in the background and caring little for Art, concerns himself solely with profits. Somebody – I do not remember who – has defined cinema as the highest form of commercial art. After ten years in this profession, I have no quarrel with that definition.... But if you are faced with a scene of a boy looking for his sister in a field of tall grass, you are faced with a particular state of mind that produces a special kind of walk, with a special kind of stopping and turning of the head. You also have to calculate the exact duration of the halt, of the exact duration of each turn of head, of the exact moment of resumption of walk..... I like to think that it was a shrewd move on my part to have selected for my first film a story where one had to put the emphasis on the human aspect.

बोनस गद्यांश 4 — RPSC LDC Exam-2012

📖 Passage — ध्यान से पढ़ें (लगभग 218 शब्द)

Have you ever, thought of the most important activity at your workplace? The answer is communication. It is an activity which consumes most of the time of an employee/employer. It is therefore imperative to make these communications effective, so that it takes lesser time of the receiver. There is always a purpose in communication. Generally the purpose of oral communication is aimed to elict and immegiateresponse. Written communication is to facilitate informed decisions and actions. Thus while the objective of oral communication is to earn an Audience, written is to earn a reader. Business writing depends on its purpose. The purpose of a Memo is to set up a meeting (internally). A letter is meant to respond to a query or put up one, a proposal to persuade (credibly) while a Report is aimed at presenting a case a problem, an analysis or a solution. What is important is to communicate effectively. This is difficult. An effective written communication involves proper structure, language, tone and style (Jargon must be kept out). Business writing is not so much a matter of creativity, rather it is a skill -subject to certain rules. Oral communication is immediate, spontaneous very often. All said and one without communication nothing will move in an organisation. Communication greases the wheels of activity in every organisation.

📝 प्रश्न 4.2: Which of the following hinders effective written communication?
Proper structure    Clarity of language    Simplicity    Use of Jargon
✅ उत्तर: Use of Jargon
📝 प्रश्न 4.3: Report writing is usually -
a proposal to persuade    a presentation of a case    a notification    a query
✅ उत्तर: a presentation of a case
📝 प्रश्न 4.4: Business writing is a matter of -
creativity    skill    imaginative planning    Adherence to principles
✅ उत्तर: skill
📝 प्रश्न 4.5: Oral communication presupposes -
Spectators    A congregation    An audience    Readers
✅ उत्तर: An audience
