Q.1राजस्थान में पर्यटन के विकास हेतु पर्यटन निदेशालय तथा पर्यटन विभाग की स्थापना क्रमशः किन वर्षों में की गई थी?
(a)1951 एवं 1952 में✗
(b)1955 एवं 1956 में✓
(c)1956 एवं 1979 में✗
(d)1952 एवं 1958 में✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
राजस्थान में पर्यटन के व्यवस्थित विकास एवं प्रशासनिक नियंत्रण हेतु वर्ष 1955 में पर्यटन निदेशालय की स्थापना की गई थी, जिसके बाद वर्ष 1956 में पृथक पर्यटन विभाग का गठन किया गया। राज्य में पर्यटकों को आवास, भोजन तथा परिवहन जैसी आधारभूत सुविधाएं प्रदान करने के उद्देश्य से 1 अप्रैल, 1979 को राजस्थान पर्यटन विकास निगम लिमिटेड (RTDC) की स्थापना की गई, जिसका मुख्यालय जयपुर में स्थित है। विकल्प A गलत है क्योंकि 1951-52 में पर्यटन से संबंधित कोई केंद्रीय संस्था स्थापित नहीं हुई थी। विकल्प C में 1979 RTDC की स्थापना का वर्ष है, न कि विभाग का। विकल्प D के वर्ष भी असंगत हैं, अतः सही उत्तर विकल्प B है।
Q.1In which years were the Tourism Directorate and Tourism Department established respectively for the development of tourism in Rajasthan?
(a)In 1951 and 1952✗
(b)In 1955 and 1956✓
(c)In 1956 and 1979✗
(d)In 1952 and 1958✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
For the systematic development and administrative control of tourism in Rajasthan, the Directorate of Tourism was established in the year 1955, after which a separate Tourism Department was formed in the year 1956. With the objective of providing basic facilities like accommodation, food and transportation to the tourists in the state, Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation Limited (RTDC) was established on April 1, 1979, whose headquarters is located in Jaipur. Option A is incorrect because no central organization related to tourism was established in 1951-52. In option C 1979 is the year of establishment of RTDC and not of the department. The years in option D are also inconsistent, hence the correct answer is option B.
Q.2राजस्थान में पर्यटन को उद्योग का दर्जा दिए जाने के संदर्भ में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:
A.पर्यटन को उद्योग का दर्जा मोहम्मद युनूस समिति की सिफारिश पर दिया गया था।
B.राजस्थान पर्यटन को उद्योग का दर्जा देने वाला देश का पहला राज्य था, जिसे 4 मार्च, 1989 को यह दर्जा मिला।
C.राजस्थान सरकार ने 18 मई, 2022 को पर्यटन एवं हॉस्पिटैलिटी क्षेत्र को पूर्ण उद्योग का दर्जा प्रदान किया।
D.राजस्थान पर्यटन विकास निगम (RTDC) की स्थापना वर्ष 1989 में की गई थी।
(a)केवल A, B और C✓
(b)केवल B, C और D✗
(c)केवल A और D✗
(d)A, B, C एवं D सभी✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
कथन A, B और C पूरी तरह सही हैं क्योंकि मोहम्मद युनूस समिति की सिफारिश पर 4 मार्च, 1989 को राजस्थान में पर्यटन को उद्योग का दर्जा दिया गया था, और 18 मई, 2022 को पर्यटन व हॉस्पिटैलिटी क्षेत्र को पूर्ण उद्योग का दर्जा दिया गया। कथन D असत्य है क्योंकि राजस्थान पर्यटन विकास निगम लिमिटेड (RTDC) की स्थापना 1 अप्रैल, 1979 को हुई थी, न कि 1989 में। विकल्प B, C और D में कथन D को शामिल किया गया है जो गलत है, इसलिए वे उत्तर नहीं हो सकते। अतः केवल A, B और C सही होने के कारण सही उत्तर विकल्प A है।
Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to tourism being given industry status in Rajasthan:
A.Tourism was given industry status on the recommendation of Mohammad Yunus Committee.
B.Rajasthan was the first state in the country to give industry status to tourism, which got this status on March 4, 1989.
C.Rajasthan government granted full industry status to the tourism and hospitality sector on May 18, 2022.
D.Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation (RTDC) was established in the year 1989.
(a)Only A, B and C✓
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Statements A, B and C are completely correct because on the recommendation of Mohammad Yunus Committee, tourism was given industry status in Rajasthan on March 4, 1989, and on May 18, 2022, tourism and hospitality sector was given full industry status. Statement D is false because Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation Limited (RTDC) was established on April 1, 1979 and not in 1989. Choices B, C and D include statement D which is false, so they cannot be the answer. Hence, the correct answer is option A since only A, B and C are correct.
Q.3राजस्थान की प्रथम पर्यटन नीति किस वर्ष घोषित की गई थी?
(a)27 सितम्बर, 2001✓
(b)1 अप्रैल, 1979✗
(c)9 सितम्बर, 2020✗
(d)4 दिसम्बर, 2024✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗

Q.3In which year was the first tourism policy of Rajasthan announced?
(a)September 27, 2001✓
(b)April 1, 1979✗
(c)September 9, 2020✗
(d)December 4, 2024✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The first tourism policy of the state was announced by the Government of Rajasthan on September 27, 2001, after which the second tourism policy was released in the year 2007. Option B (April 1, 1979) is the date of establishment of Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation (RTDC), hence this option is incorrect. Option C (September 9, 2020) is the date of implementation of the new Rajasthan Tourism Policy. Option D (December 4, 2024) indicates the date of implementation of Rajasthan Tourism Unit Policy (RTUP). Hence, the correct announcement date of the first tourism policy is option A.
Q.4राजस्थान की नवीन पर्यटन नीतियों से संबंधित निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:
A.नवीन 'राजस्थान पर्यटन नीति-2020' की अधिसूचना 9 सितम्बर, 2020 को लागू की गई।
B.नवीन 'राजस्थान ग्रामीण पर्यटन नीति' 24 नवम्बर, 2022 को लागू की गई थी।
C.'राजस्थान पर्यटन इकाई नीति (RTUP)' 4 दिसम्बर, 2024 को लागू हुई।
D.'राजस्थान पर्यटन नीति-2025' का शुभारम्भ 10 दिसम्बर, 2025 को जयपुर में किया गया।
(a)केवल A और B✗
(b)केवल B और C✗
(c)केवल A, C और D✗
(d)A, B, C एवं D सभी✓

Q.4Consider the following statements related to the new tourism policies of Rajasthan:
A.The notification of the new 'Rajasthan Tourism Policy-2020' was implemented on September 9, 2020.
B.The new 'Rajasthan Rural Tourism Policy' was implemented on November 24, 2022.
C.'Rajasthan Tourism Unit Policy (RTUP)' came into effect on December 4, 2024.
D.'Rajasthan Tourism Policy-2025' was launched on December 10, 2025 in Jaipur.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
New policies have been issued from time to time for the overall development of tourism in the state. The new Rajasthan Tourism Policy was notified on September 9, 2020, while the Rural Tourism Policy was implemented on November 24, 2022 to promote tourism in rural areas. Apart from this, Rajasthan Tourism Unit Policy was launched on December 4, 2024 and Rajasthan Tourism Policy-2025 was launched from Jaipur on December 10, 2025. Since all four statements given in the question are completely true from administrative and historical point of view, hence the correct answer is option D.

Q.5What is the time period fixed for the new Eco-Tourism Policy in Rajasthan?
(a)From February 4, 2010 to 2020✗
(b)From July 15, 2021 to 2030✓
(c)From September 9, 2020 to 2025✗
(d)From April 18, 2022 to 2028✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
To promote tourism along with natural environment and wildlife conservation in Rajasthan, a new eco-tourism policy was released on July 15, 2021, which will remain effective till the year 2030. Earlier, the first eco-tourism policy of the state was released on February 4, 2010, hence option A is the date of the first policy. In option C, September 9, 2020 is the date of general Rajasthan tourism policy and not of eco-tourism policy. Option D (April 18, 2022) pertains to film tourism policy. Hence the correct answer is option B.
Q.6'राजस्थान फिल्म पर्यटन प्रोत्साहन नीति-2022' के संदर्भ में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:
A.इस नीति को 6 अप्रैल, 2022 को मंजूरी दी गई तथा 18 अप्रैल, 2022 को लागू किया गया।
B.इसके तहत आवेदन के 15 दिनों के भीतर सभी स्वीकृतियां प्राप्त करने के लिए डिजिटल पारिस्थितिकी तंत्र का प्रावधान है।

D.नीति के निर्धारित मानदंडों को पूरा करने वाली परियोजनाओं के लिए ₹2 करोड़ तक के अनुदान का प्रावधान है।
(a)केवल A, B और D✗

(d)A, B, C एवं D सभी✓

Q.6Consider the following statements with reference to 'Rajasthan Film Tourism Promotion Policy-2022':
A.This policy was approved on April 6, 2022 and implemented on April 18, 2022.
B.Under this, there is a provision for digital ecosystem to get all the approvals within 15 days of the application.
C.A provision of 50% discount has been made on the accommodation of film units in the properties of RTDC and RSH
D.There is a provision for a grant of up to ₹2 crore for projects meeting the prescribed criteria of the policy.
(a)Only A, B and D✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A and C✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Rajasthan Film Tourism Promotion Policy-2022 was brought to develop Rajasthan as a film shooting destination at national and international level. After approval by the Cabinet on April 6, 2022, it was implemented on April 18, 2022, which provides for digital NOC within 15 days, 50% subsidy/concession in RTDC/RSHC properties and financial assistance up to ₹ 2 crore for eligible projects. There is also a provision for coordination with the Center for opening a branch of FTII. All four statements are completely true, hence option D is the correct answer.

Q.7Under the Rajasthan Film Tourism Promotion Policy-2022, what is the maximum amount of subsidy (grant) made to domestic and foreign film producers?
(a)Up to ₹ 8 crore✗
(b)Up to ₹ 6 crore✗
(c)Up to ₹ 4 crore✗
(d)Up to ₹ 2 crore✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Under the Rajasthan Film Tourism Promotion Policy-2022, a clear provision has been made to give financial subsidy/grant up to a maximum of ₹ 2 crore to the producers to promote the shooting of films in Rajasthani or other languages ​​in the state. The figures for option A (₹ 8 crore), option B (₹ 6 crore) and option C (₹ 4 crore) exceed the limits prescribed in the policy, hence these options are false. This subsidy limit has been kept at ₹ 2 crore for the purpose of promoting hospitality and logistics in the state, hence the correct answer is option D.
