Q.1In which years was the post of Divisional Commissioner abolished and reinstated in Rajasthan?
(a)Ended in 1966 and reinstated in 1973✗
(b)Abolished in 1962 and restored in 1987✓
(c)Abolished in 1962 and reinstated in 1971✗
(d)Abolished in 1959 and restored in 1987✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The divisional system in Rajasthan was started on March 30, 1949 during the tenure of Chief Minister Hiralal Shastri. Initially, 5 divisions (Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Udaipur and Bikaner) were created in the state, which included a total of 25 districts. In April 1962, the then Chief Minister Mohanlal Sukhadia completely abolished this divisional commissioner system. After this, this system was restarted (reestablished) by the Haridev Joshi government on January 26, 1987. On this occasion, Ajmer was separated from Jaipur and declared the sixth division of the state. Options A, C and D are incorrect with reference to the given dates and historical events.
