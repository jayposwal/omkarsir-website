Q.1Consider the following statements regarding the constitutional provisions of the Chief Minister and the Council of Ministers of Rajasthan:
A.According to Article 163, there will be a Council of Ministers to aid and advise the Governor with the Chief Minister as its head.
B.Under Article 164(1), the Chief Minister is appointed by the Governor and other ministers are appointed on the advice of the Chief Minister.
C.According to Article 164(2), the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Governor of the state.
D.According to Article 164(5), the salaries and allowances of ministers are determined by law by the State Legislature.
(a)Only A, B and D✓
(b)Only A, C and D✗
(c)Only B and C✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Article 163 of the Constitution provides for a Council of Ministers headed by the Chief Minister to advise the Governor on matters other than discretionary matters. Under Article 164(1), the Chief Minister is appointed by the Governor and other ministers are appointed on the advice of the Chief Minister. Under Article 164(2), the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the 'Legislative Assembly' of the state and not to the Governor. Article 164(5) clearly mentions that the salaries and allowances of ministers are determined by law by the State Legislature. Thus, statements A, B and D are true while statement C is false.

Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to the provisions added by the 91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003:
A.Clauses (1A) and clauses (1B) were inserted in Article 164 by this amendment.
B.According to this, the total number of ministers including the Chief Minister in the Council of Ministers of any state will not exceed 15% of the total number of members of the Legislative Assembly.
C.For small states, the minimum number of ministers including the Chief Minister in the Council of Ministers has been fixed at 12.
D.Depending on the assembly seats in Rajasthan, there can be a maximum of 30 and minimum 12 ministers including the Chief Minister.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Through the 91st Constitutional Amendment 2003, the size of the Council of Ministers of the states was limited and Article 164 (1A) was added. Under this, the size of the Council of Ministers can be only a maximum of 15% of the total number of members of the State Legislative Assembly (including the Chief Minister). Also, the minimum number of ministers including the Chief Minister in any state will not be less than 12. After taking out 15% of the total 200 seats of Rajasthan Assembly, there can be a maximum of 30 ministers and the minimum limit is fixed at 12 ministers. Under this amendment, a member disqualified under the Anti-Defection Law (10th Schedule) was banned from becoming a minister. Hence all the statements are true.

Q.4If the Chief Minister of Rajasthan is a member of the State Legislative Council (in case of a bicameral system), then consider the following statements regarding his rights:
A.He is not eligible to vote in the presidential election.
B.They cannot vote on the no-confidence motion brought against them in the state assembly.
C.They can announce government policies only in the Legislative Council and not in the Assembly.
D.They can recommend the dissolution of the Assembly to the Governor.
(a)Only A, B and D✓
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A and C✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
According to Article 54, the electoral college for the President includes only 'elected' members of the Parliament and Legislative Assemblies, hence the Chief Minister, a member of the Legislative Council, cannot vote in the Presidential election. No-confidence motion is brought only in the Legislative Assembly (Lower House) and voting on it is done only by the Assembly members, hence the Legislative Council member cannot vote for the CM. However, the Chief Minister, as leader of the House, can announce the policies of the government on the floor of the Assembly and can also recommend the dissolution of the Assembly to the Governor. Hence, statements A, B and D are correct while C is wrong.
Q.5राज्य में गैर-सदस्यीय मंत्री/मुख्यमंत्री के संबंध में संवैधानिक प्रावधानों के अनुसार निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा कथन पूर्णतः सत्य है?
(a)कोई भी व्यक्ति जो विधानमण्डल का सदस्य नहीं है, उसे मुख्यमंत्री नियुक्त ही नहीं किया जा सकता।✗
(b)यदि गैर-सदस्य 6 माह के भीतर विधानमण्डल की सदस्यता प्राप्त करने में विफल रहता है, तो वह पुनः उसी पद पर तुरंत नियुक्त हो सकता है।✗

Q.5Which of the following statements is completely true as per the constitutional provisions regarding the non-member minister/Chief Minister in the state?
(a)Any person who is not a member of the legislature cannot be appointed Chief Minister.✗
(b)If the non-member fails to obtain membership of the Legislature within 6 months, he can be immediately re-appointed to the same post.✗
(c)It is mandatory for a non-member minister to become a member of either house of the state legislature (Legislative Assembly or Legislative Council) within 6 months.✓
(d)A non-member minister acquires the right to vote in the Assembly as soon as he assumes office.✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Article 164(4) makes it clear that a person who is not a member of the State Legislature can also be appointed Chief Minister or Minister. But it is mandatory for him to become a member of the Legislature (any House in bicameral states) within a continuous period of 6 months from the date of assuming office. If he does not get membership within 6 months, he has to leave the post and according to the decisions of the Supreme Court, he cannot become a minister again in the same term without winning the election. Also, until he becomes a member, he has the right to speak in the House (Article 177) but does not have the right to vote.
