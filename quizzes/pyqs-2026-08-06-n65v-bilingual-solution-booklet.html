Q.1उदयपुर की पिछोला झील एवं जलमहलों से संबंधित निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:

व्याख्या

Q.1Consider the following statements related to Pichola Lake and Water Palaces of Udaipur:
A.This lake was constructed by a nomad during the reign of Maharana Lakha in the 14th century.
B.Sisarma and Bujhada rivers fall into it.
C.The construction of Jagmandir Palace located in the lake was started by Maharana Karan Singh and completed by Jagat Singh I.
D.The narrow lake connecting Lake Pichola to Fatehsagar Lake is called Swaroop Sagar.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The famous Pichola Lake of Udaipur was built in the 14th century during the reign of Maharana Lakha by a nomad named Chhitarmal in memory of his bull. Sisarma and Bujhada rivers discharge water into this lake. Among the two major water palaces located inside the lake, the construction of Jagmandir was started by Maharana Karan Singh in 1620, which was completed by Jagat Singh I in 1651, while Jaganivas Mahal was built by Jagat Singh II in 1746. During heavy rainfall, the narrow lake connecting Pichola and Fatehsagar is called Swaroop Sagar. The famous temple of Shri Mansapurna Karni Mata is situated on Dudh Talai Lake and Machhala Magra hill near Pichola Lake.

Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to Rajsamand Lake:
A.This lake was constructed by Maharana Raj Singh by blocking the water of Gomti River during famine relief work.
B.The north-eastern side of the lake is called 'Nau Chowki', where the royal eulogy is engraved on 25 black marble stones.
C.This is the world's largest praise, which was composed by Ranchod Bhatt Tailang in Sanskrit language.
D.This lake was used as a seaplane base during World War II.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Rajsamand Lake was constructed by Maharana Raj Singh of Mewar between 1662 to 1676 AD as part of famine relief work by blocking the water of Gomti River. 'Rajprashasti' is engraved in Sanskrit language on 25 large black marble stones on the nine outposts of this lake, the creator of which was Ranchod Bhatt Tailang. This is considered to be the largest inscription/citation in the world. The lake was used as a water-seaplane base for Imperial Airways and later the Royal Air Force for about six years during World War II. This lake along with Jaisamand is mainly used for irrigation in Rajasthan.

Q.3Consider the statements related to the famous Anasagar Lake of Ajmer:
A.It was constructed by Prithviraj Chauhan's grandfather Arnoraj (Anaji) around 1135 A
B.After the massacre of the Turkish army, the water of Chandra and Bandi rivers was stopped to clean the blood stained land.
C.Mughal emperor Shahjahan had built 5 marble pavilions on the banks of this lake.
D.The 'Seven Wonders Park' built under Smart City in the catchment area of ​​this lake was demolished on the orders of the court in September 2025.
(a)Only A, B and C✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Anasagar Lake is a famous artificial lake located between Nagpahar and Taragarh in Ajmer, which was constructed by Arnoraj (Anaji Chauhan) by blocking the water of Chandra and Bandi rivers to clean the blood stained land after the massacre of Turkish invaders. Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan built 5 white marble courtyards on the banks of the lake in 1637 AD, while Jahangir built Daulat Bagh (Subhash Garden) here. The country's first electric cruise will be operated in it from October 2024. The illegal 'Seven Wonders Park' built in its wetland area was demolished in September 2025 on the orders of the Supreme Court and NGT.

Q.5Consider the following statements about Pushkar Lake:
A.It is a natural freshwater volcanically formed Kaladera lake, which is spread in the shape of a crescent.
B.This lake is surrounded by a total of 52 ghats and is called 'Mother of Tirthas' and 'Pancham Tirtha'.
C.To commemorate Queen Mary's visit to India in 1911, 'Queen Mary Janana Ghat' was built here, which is now called 'Gandhi Ghat'.
D.In the year 1997-98, a cleaning program was run with the help of Canada to keep this lake clean.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Pushkar Lake situated in Ajmer is considered to be the most sacred lake of Rajasthan from religious point of view and Kaladera (volcanic) lake from natural point of view. Pushkarna was named Pushkar because it was dug by Brahmins. There are 52 ghats built around this lake. On the arrival of Queen Mary of England in 1911, a separate Janana Ghat was built for women, where it is called 'Gandhi Ghat' after the ashes of Mahatma Gandhi were immersed in it in 1948. A huge colorful fair is held here on Kartik Purnima. In 1997-98, the restoration and cleaning work of Pushkar Lake was completed with the financial and technical assistance of Canada.
