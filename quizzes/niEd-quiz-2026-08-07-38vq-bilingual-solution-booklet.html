Q.1भारत में निर्धनता रेखा के निर्धारण हेतु छठवीं पंचवर्षीय योजना में स्वीकार किए गए न्यूनतम कैलोरी उपभोग मानदंड के अनुसार ग्रामीण एवं शहरी क्षेत्रों के लिए क्रमशः प्रतिदिन प्रति व्यक्ति कितनी कैलोरी निर्धारित की गई है?
(a)2100 कैलोरी एवं 2400 कैलोरी✗
(b)2400 कैलोरी एवं 2100 कैलोरी✓
(c)2200 कैलोरी एवं 2100 कैलोरी✗
(d)2500 कैलोरी एवं 2200 कैलोरी✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
छठवीं पंचवर्षीय योजना में निर्धनता रेखा को न्यूनतम पौषणिक आवश्यकताओं के आधार पर परिभाषित किया गया था, जिसके अंतर्गत ग्रामीण क्षेत्रों में प्रति व्यक्ति प्रतिदिन 2400 कैलोरी तथा शहरी क्षेत्रों में 2100 कैलोरी का उपभोग स्तर प्राप्त करने हेतु आवश्यक धनराशि को निर्धनता रेखा माना गया। ग्रामीण क्षेत्रों में अधिक शारीरिक श्रम की आवश्यकता के कारण कैलोरी का स्तर शहरी क्षेत्रों की तुलना में अधिक रखा गया है। विकल्प A में ग्रामीण और शहरी कैलोरी मानों को परस्पर बदलकर लिखा गया है, जो कि गलत है। विकल्प C और D में दिए गए आंकड़े योजना आयोग द्वारा स्वीकृत मानकों से मेल नहीं खाते हैं। निर्धनता वह स्थिति है जिसमें व्यक्ति अपनी बुनियादी जीवन आवश्यकताओं जैसे भोजन, वस्त्र और आवास को पूरा करने में असमर्थ होता है, और भारत में इसके मापन का मुख्य आधार उपभोग व्यय तथा कैलोरी ग्रहण क्षमता रहा है।
Q.1According to the minimum calorie consumption criteria accepted in the Sixth Five Year Plan for determining the poverty line in India, how many calories per person per day have been prescribed for rural and urban areas respectively?
(a)2100 calories and 2400 calories✗
(b)2400 calories and 2100 calories✓
(c)2200 calories and 2100 calories✗
(d)2500 calories and 2200 calories✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
In the Sixth Five Year Plan, the poverty line was defined on the basis of minimum nutritional requirements, under which the amount required to achieve the consumption level of 2400 calories per day per person in rural areas and 2100 calories in urban areas was considered as poverty line. Due to the need for more physical labor in rural areas, calorie levels are kept higher than in urban areas. In option A, rural and urban calorie values ​​are written interchangeably, which is incorrect. The data given in options C and D do not match the standards accepted by the Planning Commission. Poverty is a condition in which a person is unable to meet his basic living needs such as food, clothing and shelter, and in India the main basis of its measurement has been consumption expenditure and calorie intake capacity.

Q.3Consider the following statements with reference to the poverty estimates updated by the Planning Commission for the year 2011-12 and the Tendulkar method:
A.The percentage of population living below the poverty line in Rajasthan in the year 2011-12 was 14.71%.
B.In the year 2011-12, the percentage of poverty in entire India was recorded at 21.92%.
C.In the year 2011-12, the percentage of poverty in rural areas of Rajasthan was less than that in urban areas.
D.According to Tendulkar estimates in the year 2004-05, the percentage of poverty in India was 37.2%.
(a)Only A, B and D✓
(b)Only A and C✗
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
According to the latest estimates of the Planning Commission for the year 2011-12, the percentage of below poverty line (BPL) population in Rajasthan was 14.71% (total poor 102.92 lakh), while the poverty ratio of India at the national level was 21.92% (total poor 2697.83 lakh). According to the Tendulkar Committee, poverty in India was 37.2% in 2004-05, which decreased to 21.9% in 2011-12. Statement C is false because in the year 2011-12 the percentage of poverty in rural areas of Rajasthan was 16.1%, whereas in urban areas it was only 9.2%, that is, poverty in rural areas was more than urban areas. Hence the correct answer is option A, in which statements A, B and D are considered true.

(b)Asian Development Bank (ADB)✗
(c)International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD)✓
(d)International Monetary Fund (IMF)✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The 'Poverty Alleviation Project in Western Rajasthan', abbreviated as M-POWER (Mitigating Poverty in Western Rajasthan), is being implemented in collaboration with the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD - International Fund for Agricultural Development) and Sir Ratan Tata Trust. The main objective of this project is to improve the livelihood of poor families in drought-prone and adverse geographical conditions districts of Western Rajasthan (like Barmer, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur, Jalore, Pali, Sirohi), empower self-help groups and promote market-based income enhancing activities. Option A (the World Bank) provides assistance in command area development and other infrastructure projects, while ADB provides assistance primarily in urban development and road infrastructure.
Q.5नीति आयोग द्वारा जारी बहुआयामी गरीबी सूचकांक (MPI) 2023 एवं प्रगति समीक्षा के संदर्भ में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:

Q.30राजस्थान में मरु विकास बोर्ड एवं नवीनतम आपदा/अभावग्रस्त आंकड़ों के संदर्भ में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:

'Desert Development Board' was established in the year 1966 in the state for the development and balancing of desert areas.
