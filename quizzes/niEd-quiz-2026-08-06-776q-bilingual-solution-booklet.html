Q.1मृदा प्रोफाइल (Soil Profile) एवं इसके घटकों से संबंधित निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:
A.मृदा प्रोफाइल की सबसे ऊपरी असंगठित परत पौधों को आधार प्रदान करती है तथा इसमें ह्यूमस की उपस्थिति पाई जाती है।
B.उपमृदा (Subsoil) परत में बड़े कंकर-पत्थर तथा कठोर Bedrock शामिल होते हैं जो ऊपरी मृदा को आधार देते हैं।
C.मृदा संगठन में आयतात्मक दृष्टि से सर्वाधिक योगदान खनिज पदार्थों (45%) का होता है, जबकि जैविक तत्व (ह्यूमस) मात्र 5% होते हैं।
D.जल एवं वायु का मृदा संगठन में समान अनुपात (25%-25%) होता है जो पौधों की जड़ों के श्वसन एवं पोषण हेतु आवश्यक है।
(a)केवल A और B✗
(b)केवल B, C और D✗
(c)केवल A, C और D✓
(d)A, B, C एवं D सभी✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
कथन A, C और D सत्य हैं, जबकि कथन B असत्य है। मृदा प्रोफाइल में सबसे ऊपरी असंगठित परत 'मृदा' (Soil) कहलाती है जिसमें पौधों के विकास हेतु जैविक तत्व (ह्यूमस) मौजूद होते हैं। उपमृदा (Subsoil) दूसरी परत होती है जिसमें बारीक कण एवं खनिज तत्व पाए जाते हैं, न कि कठोर Bedrock। Bedrock (आधारभूत चट्टानें) मृदा प्रोफाइल की सबसे निचली चतुर्थ परत होती है। मृदा के आदर्श संगठन में 45% खनिज तत्व, 25% जल, 25% वायु तथा 5% जैविक पदार्थ (ह्यूमस) का अनुपात पाया जाता है। भले ही ह्यूमस की मात्रा केवल 5% होती है, लेकिन यह मृदा की उर्वरा शक्ति का मुख्य निर्धारण करती है।
Q.1Consider the following statements related to soil profile and its components:
A.The uppermost unorganized layer of the soil profile provides support to plants and presence of humus is found in it.
B.The subsoil layer includes large pebbles and hard bedrock which provide support to the upper soil.
C.Mineral substances (45%) contribute the most quantitatively to soil organization, while organic elements (humus) constitute only 5%.
D.Water and air have equal proportion (25%-25%) in the soil organization which is necessary for respiration and nutrition of plant roots.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✓
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Statements A, C and D are true, while statement B is false. The uppermost unorganized layer in the soil profile is called 'soil' in which organic elements (humus) are present for the growth of plants. Subsoil is the second layer in which fine particles and minerals are found, not hard bedrock. Bedrock is the lowest quaternary layer of the soil profile. The ideal composition of soil is found to be in the ratio of 45% mineral elements, 25% water, 25% air and 5% organic matter (humus). Even though the amount of humus is only 5%, it mainly determines the fertility of the soil.

Q.2Select the true statement regarding Pedology and soil organization:
(a)The total combined volume of water and air in soil is 60%.✗
(b)The study of unorganized fine particles formed by the breakdown of rocks is called pedology.✓
(c)The amount of humus in soil is 25% which reduces the water holding capacity.✗
(d)ICAR was established in Jaipur in the year 1952 for soil research in India.✗

Explanation
The study of fine particles of unorganized rocks found on the upper surface of the earth and their physical and chemical properties is called soil science or pedology. Option A is incorrect because the combined volume of water (25%) and air (25%) is 50%. Option C is incorrect because organic matter/humus is only 5% which increases the fertility and water holding capacity of the soil. Option D is false because ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) was established in 1929 in New Delhi and not in 1952 in Jaipur.

Q.40Consider the following statements regarding the interrelationship of soil, climate and agriculture in the geography of Rajasthan:
A.Black soil is found in Hadoti region with very humid climate, which is the basis of soybean and cotton agriculture.
B.Sandy and Aridisol soils are found in the western desert with dry climate where millet, moong and moth crops are grown with less water.
C.Alluvial (Alfisol) soil is found in the eastern plains with sub-humid and humid climate, which is the leader in the production of mustard and wheat.
D.The regional distribution of soil in Rajasthan is directly controlled by the dryness of the climate and the amount of rainfall.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
All the statements (A, B, C and D) are completely true. The regional nature of soil in Rajasthan is deeply related to the climate (rainfall and temperature) there. Due to extreme dryness in the west, the particles remain coarse and sandy/eridisol soil is formed in which millet and moth grow. In the eastern plains, moderate rainfall forms alluvial/alfisol soil which is famous for wheat and mustard. In the south-east, high rainfall and lava rocks form black/vertisol soil which is ideal for cotton-soybean.
