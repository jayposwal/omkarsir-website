Q.1राजस्थान में औद्योगिक विकास को गति देने वाली तथा मध्यम व बड़े पैमाने के उद्योगों को दीर्घकालिक ऋण एवं आधारभूत ढाँचा प्रदान करने वाली शीर्षस्थ संस्था कौनसी है?
(a)राजस्थान वित्त निगम (RFC)✗
(b)राजस्थान राज्य औद्योगिक विकास एवं विनियोजन निगम (RIICO)✓
(c)राजस्थान लघु उद्योग निगम लिमिटेड (RAJSICO)✗
(d)राष्ट्रीय कृषि और ग्रामीण विकास बैंक (NABARD)✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
राजस्थान में औद्योगिक विकास को गति देने वाली शीर्षस्थ संस्था राजस्थान राज्य औद्योगिक विकास एवं विनियोजन निगम लिमिटेड (RIICO) है। इसकी स्थापना मूल रूप से 28 मार्च, 1969 को राजस्थान राज्य उद्योग एवं खनिज विकास लिमिटेड के रूप में की गई थी, जिसे जनवरी 1980 में पुनर्गठित कर वर्तमान रीको (RIICO) नाम दिया गया। इसका मुख्यालय जयपुर में स्थित है। रीको का मुख्य उद्देश्य राज्य में तीव्र औद्योगिक विकास हेतु सुदृढ़ आधारभूत संरचना का निर्माण करना है, जिसके अंतर्गत यह लघु विकास केंद्रों, सेज (SEZ), तथा निर्यात संवर्धन औद्योगिक पार्कों की स्थापना करता है। इसके अतिरिक्त यह मध्यम एवं बड़े पैमाने के उद्योगों को दीर्घकालिक वित्तीय सहायता एवं ऋण उपलब्ध कराने का कार्य भी करता है।
Q.1Which is the apex institution that accelerates industrial development in Rajasthan and provides long-term loans and infrastructure to medium and large scale industries?
(a)Rajasthan Finance Corporation (RFC)✗
(b)Rajasthan State Industrial Development and Investment Corporation (RIICO)✓
(c)Rajasthan Small Industries Corporation Limited (RAJSICO)✗
(d)National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The apex body responsible for accelerating industrial development in Rajasthan is Rajasthan State Industrial Development and Investment Corporation Limited (RIICO). It was originally established on March 28, 1969 as Rajasthan State Industries and Mineral Development Limited, which was reorganized in January 1980 and given the current name RIICO. Its headquarters is located in Jaipur. The main objective of RIICO is to create strong infrastructure for rapid industrial development in the state, under which it establishes small development centres, SEZs, and export promotion industrial parks. Apart from this, it also works to provide long-term financial assistance and loans to medium and large scale industries.

Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to the establishment and reorganization of Rajasthan State Industrial Development and Investment Corporation (RIICO):
A.RIICO was originally established on March 28, 1969 as Rajasthan State Industries and Mineral Development Limited.
B.In January 1980, Mineral Development was separated and reorganized under the present name 'RICO'.
C.Its headquarters is located in Jodhpur.
D.It is the main central agency for the development of SEZs and industrial parks in the state.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only A, B and D✓
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
RIICO was established on March 28, 1969 as 'Rajasthan State Industries and Mineral Development Corporation Limited' (RSIMDC). In January 1980, 'Rajasthan State Mines and Minerals Limited' (RSMM) was formed by separating the mineral development sector and the present RIICO came into existence for industrial development. Its headquarters is located in Jaipur and not Jodhpur, hence statement C is false. RIICO is the apex agency for developing Special Economic Zones (SEZs), Agro Food Parks and Export Promotion Parks in the state. Hence, the correct answer is option B (A, B and D only).

Q.3Which is the organization that operates 'Rajasthali' outlets for raw material, financial assistance and marketing of manufactured products to small scale industries and handicraftsmen in Rajasthan?
(a)RIICO✗
(b)Rajasthan Finance Corporation (RFC)✗
(c)Rajasthan Small Industries Corporation Limited (RAJSICO)✓
(d)Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation (RTDC)✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Rajasthan Small Industries Corporation Limited (RAJSICO) was established on June 3, 1961 under the Indian Companies Act, 1956. Its main objective is to provide adequate incentives, raw materials, training and marketing facilities to small industrial units and handicraftsmen in the state. Rajsico procures the items directly from the artisans and runs 'Rajasthali' emporiums/outlets for their promotion and sale. Apart from this, Rajsico has also established International Trade Zone, so that the artisans of the state can get fair prices for their products.

Explanation
To strengthen the export infrastructure in Rajasthan, a total of 8 dry ports (inland container depots) are operated. Q.6What is the full name of RUIDP, a scheme being run since January 2000 with the help of Asian Development Bank (ADB) to increase drinking water and transportation capacities in 6 major cities of Rajasthan?
(a)Rajasthan Urban Infrastructure Finance Development Corporation✗
(b)Rajasthan Urban Infrastructure Development Project✓
(c)Rajasthan Urban Rural Development Program✗
(d)Rajasthan Urban Health System Development Project✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The full form of RUIDP is 'Rajasthan Urban Infrastructure Development Project'. This project is being run with the financial assistance of Asian Development Bank (ADB) since January 18, 2000 in 6 major cities of the state - Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kota, Ajmer, Bikaner and Udaipur. Its main objective is the overall socio-economic development of urban areas and expansion of sewerage, transport and water supply schemes. In this, the Central Government provides 70% amount at variable interest rate and 30% amount to the State Government in the form of grant, the repayment period of which is 25 years.
