Q.1राज्य सरकार द्वारा प्रथम पशुधन विकास नीति की घोषणा कब की गई थी?
(a)26 जनवरी, 2010✗
(b)17 फरवरी, 2010✓
(c)15 अगस्त, 2010✗
(d)1 अप्रैल, 2010✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
राज्य मन्त्रिमण्डल द्वारा 17 फरवरी, 2010 को राजस्थान की प्रथम 'पशुधन विकास नीति' लागू की गई थी। इस नीति का मुख्य उद्देश्य राज्य में पशुधन क्षेत्र का सतत विकास करना, पशुओं की नस्ल सुधारना, पशु स्वास्थ्य सुविधाओं में विस्तार करना तथा पशुपालकों की सामाजिक-आर्थिक स्थिति को सुदृढ़ बनाना था। विकल्प A गलत है क्योंकि 26 जनवरी को यह नीति घोषित नहीं हुई थी। विकल्प C में 15 अगस्त, 2012 को मुख्यमंत्री पशुधन निःशुल्क दवा योजना शुरू हुई थी, न कि पशुधन नीति। विकल्प D में 1 अप्रैल, 2010 को कोई विशेष राज्य पशुधन नीति लागू नहीं हुई थी। अतः सही उत्तर विकल्प B (17 फरवरी, 2010) है।
Q.1When was the first Livestock Development Policy announced by the State Government?
(a)January 26, 2010✗
(b)February 17, 2010✓
(c)August 15, 2010✗
(d)April 1, 2010✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The first 'Livestock Development Policy' of Rajasthan was implemented by the State Cabinet on February 17, 2010. The main objective of this policy was to sustainably develop the livestock sector in the state, improve the breed of animals, expand animal health facilities and strengthen the socio-economic condition of livestock farmers. Option A is incorrect because this policy was not announced on January 26. In option C, the Chief Minister Livestock Free Medicine Scheme was launched on August 15, 2012, not the Livestock Policy. In option D, no special state livestock policy was implemented on April 1, 2010. Hence, the correct answer is option B (February 17, 2010).

Q.3Consider the following statements related to processing, marketing and camel conservation of camel milk in Rajasthan:
A.In the Budget 2018-19, it was announced to set up a mini plant for processing and marketing of camel milk in Jaipur.
B.Provision was made to establish 'Camel Conservation Centre' in Jaipur for the conservation and promotion of camels.
C.In the year 2024, camel milk was launched under the brand name 'Amul' by the state dairy department.
D.Rajasthan Camel (Prohibition of Slaughter and Regulation of Temporary Migration or Export) Bill was passed in the year 2015.
(a)Only A, B and D✓
(b)Only A, C and D✗
(c)Only B and C✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
In the state budget 2018-19, it was announced to set up a mini plant and camel conservation center in Jaipur for processing and marketing of camel milk. Also, after declaring camels as state animal (30 June 2014), the Camel Slaughter Prohibition Bill was passed in 2015. Statement C is incorrect because in the year 2024, camel milk was launched by the Minister of Animal Husbandry and Dairy under the brand name 'Saras' and not by the name Amul. Amul is the dairy brand of Gujarat while Saras is the authorized brand of Rajasthan Cooperative Dairy Federation (RCDF). Hence the correct statements are A, B and D.

Q.6Consider the following statements related to camel diseases and ornaments:
A.'Sarra' or 'Tibarsa' disease is the main disease of camels caused by protozoa (Trypanosoma ivanaceae).
B.Because the duration of this disease lasts for about 3 years, it is called 'Tibarsa' in the local language.
C.'Girban' is a wooden neck ornament worn in the nose of a camel.
D.The main ornament of the camel's neck is called 'Gorband'.
(a)Only A and C✗
(b)Only B and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
'Sarrah' disease in camels is caused by a protozoa called Trypanosoma ivansai. This disease persists in the camel's body for about three years, that is why it is called 'Tibarsa' (three-year disease). The wooden handle cut into the nose of a camel to control it is called 'Girbaan'. The famous ornament tied around the neck of the camel for decoration is 'Gorband', on which the famous Rajasthani folk song is also popular. Hence, all four given statements are true, hence option D is the correct answer.
