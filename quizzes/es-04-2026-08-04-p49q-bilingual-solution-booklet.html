Q.1Consider the following statements with reference to the 'Developed Rajasthan @2047' vision:
A.Its main goal is to make Rajasthan an equitable, inclusive and prosperous state by the year 2047.
B.It focuses only on the economic empowerment of Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST).
C.This includes creating a resilient workforce and skilled youth.
D.It emphasizes on providing global excellence and national identity to the sports talents of the state.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only A, C and D✓
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Statements A, C and D are completely correct, while statement B is incorrect. The vision of 'Developed Rajasthan @2047' includes overall social, political and economic empowerment of not only SCs and STs, but also of all the weak, deprived and sensitive sections of the society - such as women, children, specially abled persons, senior citizens and minorities. Statement B shows limited scope which is factually incorrect. Hence the correct option is B (A, C and D only).

Q.5How much total amount of financial assistance is provided to the eligible girl child under the 'Lado Protsahan Yojana' implemented by the Rajasthan Government from August 1, 2024?

Under the 'Lado Protsahan Yojana' effective from August 1, 2024, a total financial assistance of ₹ 1.50 lakh is provided in 7 installments on the birth of an eligible girl child by the Rajasthan Government. Q.6Consider the following statements in the context of 'Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana':
A.Basic assistance amount of ₹ 31,000 is given on the marriage of a girl above 18 years of age from SC, ST and minority class BPL families.
B.If the girl has passed 10th then an additional incentive of ₹ 10,000 is given and if she has passed graduation, an additional incentive of ₹ 20,000 is given.
C.Basic assistance of ₹ 21,000 is provided to the daughters of other BPL and Antyodaya/Aastha card holding families.
D.The minimum age limit in this scheme has been fixed at 21 years.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only A, B and C✓
(c)Only C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Statements A, B and C are correct, but statement D is incorrect. Under Mukhyamantri Kanyadan Yojana, the minimum age limit of a girl for marriage is fixed at 18 years (not 21 years). Girls from SC/ST/minority BPL families are given ₹31,000 on marriage and depending on educational qualification the maximum assistance ranges from ₹21,000 to ₹51,000. In other categories (like General BPL, Widow, Divyangjan Family) the basic assistance is ₹21,000 which goes up to a maximum of ₹41,000 after adding educational additional benefits. Hence option B is the correct answer.
