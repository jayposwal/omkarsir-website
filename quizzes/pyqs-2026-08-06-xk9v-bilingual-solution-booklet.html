Q.1राजस्थान की जलवायु को प्रभावित करने वाला मुख्य आधारभूत तत्त्व निम्नलिखित में से कौनसा है?

Q.1Which of the following is the main basic element affecting the climate of Rajasthan?
(a)distance from sea✗
(b)height above sea level✗
(c)temperature✓
(d)vegetation cover✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option C. Temperature is considered to be the most important factor influencing the climate of Rajasthan, because extreme extremes in temperature (up to a maximum of 49°C) and high daily and annual temperature variations are found in the state. This variation in temperature controls air pressure, wind patterns and evaporation rates. Although distance from the sea, relief and position of Aravali are also helpful factors, but temperature plays a primary role in determining the basic nature of climate. Vegetation cover and relief are secondary factors.

Q.2Most of the surface and highland parts of Rajasthan fall in the category of how many meters above mean sea level?
(a)150 to 200 meters✗
(b)200 to 250 meters✗
(c)250 to 300 meters✓
(d)450 to 500 meters✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option C. Most of the land area of ​​Rajasthan is situated at an altitude of less than 300 meters above mean sea level, which mainly falls in the range of 250 to 300 meters. Due to such low altitude (low relief), the ocean leveling effect does not reach here and high temperatures persist. Heights of 450-500 meters or more are found only in the hilly areas of the Aravalli mountain range. Therefore, the plains and desert part of the state represent low relief range.

Q.4Consider the following statements with reference to the climatic conditions of Rajasthan:
A.The climatic conditions of Rajasthan are characterized by extremes of temperature and rainfall.
B.Most of Rajasthan falls in the tropical zone.
C.The amount of rainfall in the state increases from south-east to north-west.
D.The entire climate of the state is placed in the extremely humid category only.
(a)Only A is correct✓
(b)Only A and B are correct✗
(c)Only C and D are correct✗
(d)A, B, C and D are all correct✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option A. The climate of Rajasthan is characterized by extreme variations in rainfall, with extreme heat in summers and intense cold in winters. Statement B is false because 99% of Rajasthan falls in the subtropical/temperate zone and only 1% (Dungarpur-Banswara) falls in the tropical zone. Statement C is false because the amount of rainfall decreases (not increases) from south-east to north-west. Statement D is false because 5 different climatic zones are found in the state ranging from dry to very humid.

Q.5Consider the following factors affecting the climate of Rajasthan:
A.Latitudinal location and latitudinal extent
B.Position of the Aravalli mountain range parallel to the monsoon winds
C.Presence of the Thar Desert and high convective activities
D.Extreme distance from the Gulf of Kutch and the Arabian Sea
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)All A, B, C and D✓
(d)Only A, B and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option C. The climate of Rajasthan is deeply influenced by these four geographical factors. Latitudinal location reflects climate diversity in temperature and latitudinal extent. The location of the Aravalis allows the Arabian Sea monsoon to move directly towards the north without causing rainfall. In desert sand soil, convection currents are formed due to rapid heating. Apart from this, due to distance from Gulf of Kutch (225 km) and Arabian Sea (400 km), continental dry climate is found.

Q.6In Rajasthan, when moving from which direction to which direction, there is a continuous increase in the amount of monsoon rainfall?
(a)south-west to north-east✗
(b)north-west to south-east✓
(c)south-east to north-west✗
(d)south to north✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option B. The direction of increase in rainfall in Rajasthan is from north-west (minimum rainfall areas like Jaisalmer) towards south-east (maximum rainfall areas like Jhalawar and Kota). On the contrary, if we move from south-east to north-west, the amount of rainfall decreases. Monsoon winds enter from Hadoti plateau and become dry as they move forward. Therefore, it correctly represents the increase rate of rainfall from north-west to south-east.

Q.7What is the general direction of monsoon winds blowing in Rajasthan during the rainy season (mid-June to September)?
(a)North South✗
(b)north-east to south-west✗
(c)south-west to north-east✓
(d)east to north✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option C. Summer monsoon winds blow from south-west to north-east direction carrying water vapor from the Indian Ocean and Arabian Sea. For this reason these winds are called 'south-west monsoon'. Winds blowing from north to south or north-east to south-west are part of the winter monsoon or retreating monsoon, which are generally dry. Therefore, the main direction of monsoon winds of the rainy season is from south-west to north-east.

Q.8What is meant by the word 'Purvaiya' or 'Parvai' used in the local language in Rajasthan?
(a)Monsoon winds of the Bay of Bengal blowing from east to west✓
(b)dry westerly winds blowing from west to east✗
(c)sea ​​winds blowing from south to north✗
(d)winter icy winds blowing from the north✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option A. When the Bay of Bengal branch of the south-west monsoon crosses the plains and enters Rajasthan from the east, these watery monsoon winds are called 'Purvaiya' or 'Parvaiya' in the local language.
