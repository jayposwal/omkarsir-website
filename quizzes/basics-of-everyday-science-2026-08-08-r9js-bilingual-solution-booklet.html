Q.1हिप्पोक्रेट्स (Hippocrates) को विज्ञान की किस शाखा का जनक माना जाता है?
(a)खगोलशास्त्र✗
(b)चिकित्सा शास्त्र✓
(c)ज्योतिष शास्त्र✗
(d)वनस्पति विज्ञान✗
(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
हिप्पोक्रेट्स को चिकित्सा शास्त्र (Medical Science) का जनक माना जाता है। उन्होंने प्राचीन यूनान में चिकित्सा को अंधविश्वास और धार्मिक मान्यताओं से अलग कर एक वैज्ञानिक और व्यावहारिक आधार प्रदान किया था। हिप्पोक्रेट्स ने ही डॉक्टरों के लिए एक नैतिक आचार संहिता तैयार की थी, जिसे आज भी 'हिप्पोक्रेटिक ओथ' (Hippocratic Oath) के रूप में नए चिकित्सकों द्वारा लिया जाता है। विकल्पों में दिए गए वनस्पति विज्ञान के जनक थियोफ्रेस्ट्स (Theophrastus) हैं, आधुनिक रसायन विज्ञान के जनक रॉबर्ट बॉयल हैं तथा जीव विज्ञान एवं जंतु विज्ञान के जनक अरस्तू हैं।
Q.1Hippocrates is considered the father of which branch of science?
(a)astronomy✗
(b)medical science✓
(c)astrology✗
(d)Botany✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Hippocrates is considered the father of Medical Science. He separated medicine in ancient Greece from superstition and religious beliefs and provided a scientific and practical basis. It was Hippocrates who prepared a code of ethical conduct for doctors, which is still taken by new doctors in the form of 'Hippocratic Oath'. The father of Botany given in the options is Theophrastus, the father of modern chemistry is Robert Boyle and the father of biology and zoology is Aristotle.

Q.3What is the technique of soilless agriculture or growing plants in water containing only nutrients called?
(a)hydrostatics✗
(b)cybernetics✗
(c)hydroponics✓
(d)Tillandsias✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Soilless agriculture or the scientific method of growing plants in a solution of only water and essential mineral nutrients without soil is called Hydroponics. This technology is a boon for areas where fertile soil is not available or there is scarcity of land. In this, the roots of the plants are kept immersed in nutrient solution and controlled supply of light and oxygen is provided. In option A, hydrostatics is related to the study of hydrostatics, in option B, cybernetics is the study of control and communication between humans and machines, and tillandsias is related to plants growing on atmospheric moisture.

Q.4Consider the following statements related to various specialized study branches of science:
A.Soil is studied under Pedology.
B.Only pollen grains are studied under Pomology.
C.Friction and lubricants are studied under Tribology.
D.Ethnobotany studies the use of plants by tribals and human races.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only A, C and D✓
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Statements A, C and D are completely true. Pedology is the branch of science that studies the formation, structure and classification of soil. Under tribology, the scientific study of friction, wear and lubricants between surfaces in contact with each other is done. Ethnobotany is a branch of botanical science that studies the traditional use of medicinal and practical plants by local tribals and human communities. Statement B is false because pomology is the study of 'fruits', whereas the study of pollen is called 'palynology'.

Q.75When there is a strong storm, light thatched or tin roofs get blown away. On which principle of physics is it based?

(c)Pascal's law✗
(d)Kepler's law✗

Explanation
According to Bernoulli's Principle, where the velocity of an ideal fluid or gas is high, the pressure decreases. When a strong storm occurs, air flows over the roof at a very high speed, due to which the atmospheric pressure above the roof becomes very low.
