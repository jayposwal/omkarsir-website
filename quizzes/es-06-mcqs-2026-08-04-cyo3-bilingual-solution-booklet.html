C.The natural and historical heritage of the state will be conserved through sustainable policies, innovation and digital integration.
D.This vision expresses a commitment to provide world-class tourism and spiritual experiences for future generations.

(b)Only A and B✗
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗

Explanation
Statements A, C and D are completely true. The other options are incorrect because option B contains a false statement, while options C and D include B which is misleading.

(b)47.71%✓
(c)52.10%✗

Explanation
The total contribution of the services sector to the GSVA of Rajasthan in 2025-26 at prevailing prices has been 47.71%, with a total value of ₹8.17 lakh crore. The figure of 52.10% is not relevant to this review. Hence the correct answer is option B.
