Q.1Consider the following statements related to the naming and historical context of Rajasthan:
A.In the Vedic period (Rigveda), the Rajasthan region was called 'Brahmavarta', which was considered the sacred cultural land of the Aryans.
B.The word 'Marukantara' is used for this inaccessible desert area in Valmiki's Ramayana and Mahabharata.
C.George Thomas made the first written use of the word 'Rajputana' in his book in the year 1800 A
D.Colonel James Todd used the word 'Rajasthan' for the first time in writing for this region in his book in 1829 AD.
(a)Only A, B and D✓
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option A, because statements A, B and D are completely true while statement C is false. George Thomas was the first to use the word 'Rajputana' verbally, not in written form, in the year 1800 AD. The first written use of the word 'Rajputana' was made by William Franklin in his book 'Military Memoirs of Mr. George Thomas' in 1805 AD. This area was called 'Brahmavarta' in Rigveda and 'Marukantara' during Ramayana and Mahabharata period. Colonel James Todd used the words 'Rajasthan', 'Rajwada' and 'Raithan' for the first time in writing in his book 'The Annals and Antiquities of Rajasthan' in 1829 AD. Hence option A is the correct answer as statement C is wrong.

Q.2Which of the following statements is false regarding Colonel James Tod and his famous work?
(a)Colonel Tod is called 'Ghode wale Baba' and 'Father of History Writing' in Rajasthan.✗
(b)The name of his famous book is 'The Annals and Antiquities of Rajasthan' (1829 AD), whose other name is 'Central and Western Rajput States of India'.✗
(c)The Hindi translation of Colonel Tod's book was first done by Kaviraja Shyamaldas.✓
(d)Colonel Tod had traveled the entire Rajputana on horseback to compile an authentic history of Rajasthan.✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option C, because the first translation of Colonel James Tod's historical book 'The Annals and Antiquities of Rajasthan' into Hindi was done by the famous historian Pandit Gaurishankar Hirachand Ojha and not by Kaviraja Shyamaldas. Colonel Tod is known as 'Ghode wale Baba' in Rajasthan because he traveled throughout the Rajputana region and collected historical documents. He is considered the father of history writing of Rajasthan. His book was published from London in 1829 AD, in which the words Raithan, Rajwada and Rajasthan were used. Hence the statement given in option C is false.

Q.4Consider the following statements regarding the geological origin of Rajasthan and Alfred Wegener's continental displacement theory:
A.German scientist Alfred Wegener presented the continental displacement theory in 1912.
B.In ancient times the entire landmass was connected as a huge continent called 'Pangaea'.
C.The huge ocean spread around Pangea was called 'Panthalassa', the present remnant of which is the Pacific Ocean.
D.The northern part of Pangea was called 'Gondwana Land' and the southern part was called 'Angara Land'.
(a)Only A, B and C✓
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option A, because statements A, B and C are true while statement D is false. According to Alfred Wegener, the northern part of Pangea was called 'Angara Land' (Laurasia), which included North America, Europe and Northern Asia, while the southern part was called 'Gondwana Land', which included South America, Africa, Peninsular India, Australia and Antarctica. In statement D, the names of the northern and southern parts have been reversed, hence this statement is false. The landmass formed in the middle of Pangea was called the Tethys Sea. Hence, option A is the correct answer since statement D is wrong.

Q.35राजस्थान के नवीनतम सांख्यिकी आंकडे (2025-26 अपडेट्स) एवं प्रशासनिक घटनाओं के संबंध में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:
A.नीति आयोग के तकनीकी समूह के अनुमानों के अनुसार जुलाई-अक्टूबर 2025 तक राजस्थान की अनुमानित जनसंख्या लगभग 8.31 से 8.33 करोड़ तक पहुँच गई है।

C.फरवरी 2026 में प्रकाशित अंतिम मतदाता सूची के अनुसार राजस्थान में पंजीकृत कुल मतदाताओं की संख्या 5.15 करोड़ से अधिक दर्ज की गई है।

Q.35Consider the following statements regarding the latest statistical data (2025-26 updates) and administrative developments of Rajasthan:
A.According to the estimates of the technical group of NITI Aayog, the estimated population of Rajasthan has reached about 8.31 to 8.33 crore by July-October 2025.
B.Based on estimated population, Rajasthan has become the 6th most populous state of India.
C.According to the final voter list published in February 2026, the total number of registered voters in Rajasthan has been recorded at more than 5.15 crore.
D.In the year 2026, Rajasthan Day celebrations were not celebrated only on one day but from 14 to 19 March 2026 as 'Rajasthan Day Week' with various events.
(a)All A, B, C and D✓
(b)Only A, B and C✗
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)Only A, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The correct answer is option A (all A, B, C and D). Due to the postponement of Census 2021, the latest data for 2025-26 is based on the projection report of the technical group of NITI Aayog. According to this, the population of Rajasthan is estimated at 8.31-8.33 crores, making Rajasthan the 6th most populous state in the country. More than 5.15 crore voters are registered in the voter list released by the Election Department in February 2026. In March 2026, under the leadership of Chief Minister Bhajan Lal Sharma, 30 March was celebrated as 'Rajasthan Day' with various cultural and development programs throughout the week from 14-19 March 2026, giving a broader form to 'Rajasthan Day'. All the statements are true.
