Q.12011 की जनगणना के संदर्भ में राजस्थान की कुल जनसंख्या तथा देश की कुल जनसंख्या में इसके प्रतिशत अंश का सही युग्म कौनसा है?

(d)8.19 करोड़ तथा 6.85%✗

राज्य जनगणना निदेशालय द्वारा 25 मई, 2013 को जारी 2011 की जनगणना के अंतिम आंकड़ों के अनुसार राजस्थान की कुल जनसंख्या 6,85,48,437 (लगभग 6.85 करोड़) दर्ज की गई, जो 2001 की जनसंख्या 5.65 करोड़ से बढ़कर प्राप्त हुई थी। देश की कुल जनसंख्या (121.09 करोड़) की दृष्टि से राजस्थान का स्थान 2011 में 8वाँ था और भारत की कुल जनसंख्या में राजस्थान का अंश 5.67 प्रतिशत था। 1 अक्टूबर, 2014 को आंध्र प्रदेश से तेलंगाना के पृथक होने के पश्चात् राजस्थान का जनसंख्या में स्थान 7वाँ हो गया था। विकल्प A में दी गई जनसंख्या 5.65 करोड़ वर्ष 2001 के जनगणना आंकड़े को दर्शाती है, जबकि विकल्प C और D के आंकड़े वास्तविक जनगणना परिणामों से मेल नहीं खाते हैं।
Q.1In the context of 2011 census, which is the correct combination of total population of Rajasthan and its percentage in the total population of the country?
(a)5.65 crore and 4.50%✗
(b)6.85 crore and 5.67%✓
(c)7.10 crore and 6.10%✗
(d)8.19 crore and 6.85%✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
According to the final data of 2011 census released by the State Census Directorate on May 25, 2013, the total population of Rajasthan was recorded at 6,85,48,437 (about 6.85 crores), which had increased from the 2001 population of 5.65 crores. In terms of the total population of the country (121.09 crore), Rajasthan ranked 8th in 2011 and its share in the total population of India was 5.67 percent. After the separation of Telangana from Andhra Pradesh on October 1, 2014, Rajasthan's position became 7th in population. The population given in option A reflects the 2001 census figure of 5.65 crore, while the figures in options C and D do not match the actual census results.

Q.2Consider the following statements related to the population size and administrative status of Rajasthan according to Census 2011:
A.Census 2011 was the 15th census of the country and the 7th after independence.
B.During the year 2001 to 2011, the population of Rajasthan increased by about 1.20 crores.
C.According to the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, the population of Rajasthan in 2011 was estimated at 121.09 crore.
D.The decision to conduct caste census in the year 2011 was taken for the first time after 1931 along with the general census.
(a)Only A and B✓
(b)Only A, B and D✗
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Statement A is correct because Census 2011 was the 15th national census of India and the 7th since independence in 1947. Statement B is also true because the population of Rajasthan was 5,65,07,188 in 2001, which increased to 6,85,48,437 in 2011, that is, there was a total decadal increase of 1,20,41,249 (about 1.20 crores). Statement C is incorrect because 121.09 crore was the total population of entire India and not of Rajasthan. Statement D is incorrect because it was decided to conduct the caste-based census not along with the general census but as a separate Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC-2011), whereas the last caste census before this was conducted in the year 1931.
