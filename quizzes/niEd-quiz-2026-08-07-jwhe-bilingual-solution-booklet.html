Q.1Consider the following statements with reference to the development of energy sector and installed capacity in Rajasthan:
A.The total installed energy capacity of the state has increased to 31,556.43 MW by December, 2025.
B.Thermal energy has the highest contribution (about 60%) in the total energy production in the state.
C.The combined contribution of wind and solar energy in Rajasthan is about 45.36% of the total capacity.
D.In December, 2021, the energy installed capacity of Rajasthan was 23,321 MW.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
According to Rajasthan Economic Review 2025-26, a rapid increase in the energy potential of the state has been recorded. As of December 2021, the installed energy capacity in Rajasthan was 23,321 MW, which has increased to 27,283.83 MW by March 2025 and to 31,556.43 MW by December 2025. The maximum contribution of thermal energy in the total energy generation mix of the state is about 60%, followed by wind and solar energy at 45.36%. Apart from this, nuclear energy and biomass also have important contribution. Thus, all four given statements are completely true, hence option D is the correct answer.

Q.3Regarding the classification of non-conventional and conventional energy sources in Rajasthan, which of the following energy sources falls in the conventional category?
(a)biomass energy✗
(b)hydroelectric power✓
(c)geothermal energy✗
(d)tidal wave energy✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Energy sources are divided into conventional and non-conventional categories on the basis of commercial and natural availability. Traditional energy sources include thermal power (coal, petroleum, natural gas), nuclear/nuclear energy and hydroelectric power. On the other hand, solar energy, wind energy, bio-gas, bio-mass, tidal wave energy and geothermal energy are called non-conventional or renewable sources. Option B is the correct answer as hydroelectric power is a conventional source, while the other three options are examples of non-conventional sources.

(b)Only B and D✗

Explanation

(d)Cookies Grid Project✗
