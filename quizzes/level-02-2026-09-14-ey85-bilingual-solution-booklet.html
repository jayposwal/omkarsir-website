Explanation
Among the major peaks of the Aravalli range, Raghunathgarh (Sikar) is the highest peak of the Northern Aravalis with a height of 1055 metres. Dora Parvat (Jalore) is the highest peak of the Jaswantpura hills with a height of 869 metres. This sequence reflects the relief of the different ranges of the Aravali, with Raghunathgarh being the only peak over 1000 meters high in the northern region.

A.बिलाली (775 मीटर) तथा बरवाड़ा (786 मीटर) उत्तरी अरावली की प्रमुख पर्वत चोटियाँ हैं।

Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to the mountain peaks of Central and Northern Aravali and their geographical location:
A.Bilali (775 m) and Barwada (786 m) are the main mountain peaks of Northern Aravali.
B.Taragarh (873 m) and Nag Pahar (795 m) are the main mountain peaks of Central Aravalli.
C.Saira (900 m) and Leelagarh (874 m) are the highest peaks of Central Aravali.
D.Harsh Parvat and Malkhet hills are located in Shekhawati region of Sikar district.
(a)Only A, B and D✓
(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A and C✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Bilali (775 m) in Alwar district and Barwada (786 m) in Jaipur Rural district are the major peaks of Northern Aravali. The Central Aravalli extend mainly in Ajmer and Beawar districts, where Taragarh (873 m) and Nag Pahar (795 m) are the major peaks. Statement C is incorrect because Saira (900 m) and Leelagarh (874 m) are not peaks of the Central Aravalli, but of the Southern Aravalli (Udaipur/Mewar hill region). The highest peak of the Central Aravalli is Gauramji/Moramji (934 m). Harsha Parvat (Jeenamata area) and Malkhet hills are located in Sikar district.

Q.4पश्चिमी राजस्थान के जालौर-बाड़मेर क्षेत्र में स्थित 56 की गोलाकार पहाड़ियाँ (नाकोड़ा पर्वत) भू-वैज्ञानिक दृष्टि से मुख्यतः किस प्रकार की शैलों से निर्मित हैं?
(a)नीस एवं बेसाल्ट शैलें✗
(b)शिस्ट एवं क्वार्टजाइट शैलें✗

व्याख्या
पश्चिमी राजस्थान में सिवाना (बालोतरा/बाड़मेर) से जालौर तक 56 की पहाड़ियाँ गोलाकार गुंबदनुमा पहाड़ियों के रूप में विस्तृत हैं, जिन्हें नाकोड़ा पर्वत भी कहा जाता है। ये पहाड़ियाँ प्री-कैम्ब्रियन काल के मालानी आग्नेय शैल समूह (Malani Igneous Suite) और जालौर ग्रेनाइट/रायलोइट चट्टानों से निर्मित हैं। ये शैलें अत्यधिक कठोर एवं अपक्षय-प्रतिरोधी हैं, जिसके कारण यहाँ 'इन्सेलवर्ग' (Inselberg) जैसी विशिष्ट स्थलाकृतियाँ मिलती हैं। जालौर को इसी कारण 'ग्रेनाइट सिटी' भी कहा जाता है। बेसाल्ट शैलें हाड़ौती पठार में तथा विन्ध्यन बलुआ पत्थर पूर्वी राजस्थान में पाए जाते हैं।
Q.4From the geological point of view, the 56 rounded hills (Nakora Mountains) located in the Jalore-Barmer region of western Rajasthan are mainly made up of which type of rocks?
(a)gneiss and basalt rocks✗
(b)Schist and quartzite rocks✗
(c)Malani Igneous Rock Group and Jalore Granite✓
(d)Sandstone of Vindhyan ledge✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
In western Rajasthan, from Siwana (Balotra/Barmer) to Jalore, the 56 hills extend in the form of rounded dome-shaped hills, which are also known as Nakoda Mountains. These hills are composed of Malani Igneous Suite and Jalore granite/rhyolite rocks of Pre-Cambrian period. These rocks are extremely hard and weathering-resistant, due to which special topographies like 'Inselberg' are found here. For this reason Jalore is also called 'Granite City'. Basalt rocks are found in Hadoti plateau and Vindhyan sandstone is found in eastern Rajasthan.
Q.5अरावली पर्वतमाला के प्रमुख प्राकृतिक दर्रों (Passes) के संदर्भ में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:

Q.5Consider the following statements with reference to the major natural passes of the Aravalli mountain range:
A.Bar Pass, Parveriya Pass and Shivpur Ghat are the major passes located on the Beawar and Pali border.
B.Desuri Naal and Someshwar Naal are narrow mountain passes connecting Pali with Rajsamand and Mewar.
C.Hathi Pass and Debari Pass are located in the Southern Aravali in Udaipur district.
D.Kamli Ghat Pass connects only Jaisalmer and Barmer districts.
(a)Only A, B and C✓
(b)Only B and D✗
(c)Only A and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The passes of Central and Southern Aravali are famous as historical traffic and strategic routes of Rajasthan. Bar Pass facilitates transportation between Beawar and Pali on the National Highway, while Shivpur Ghat and Parveriya Pass are located in Beawar region. Desuri Naal and Someshwar Naal connect the Desuri area of ​​Pali district with Mewar (Rajsamand/Kumbhalgarh). Hathi Pass and Debari Pass are the major passes of Udaipur district, where Debari has been the historical gateway to Mewar. Statement D is false because Kamli Ghat Pass is a mountain pass located on the border of Rajsamand and Pali, not in the desert Jaisalmer-Barmer.
