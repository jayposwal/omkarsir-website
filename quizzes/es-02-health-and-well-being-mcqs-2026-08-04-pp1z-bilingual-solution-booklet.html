Q.1Consider the following statements in the context of Rajasthan's developed Rajasthan@2047 health vision and legal framework:
A.The state aims to achieve optimal physical, mental, social and spiritual well-being for all citizens by the year 2047.
B.The National Health Policy 2017 recommends spending 2/3 of the health budget on PHC to strengthen primary health care.
C.Rajasthan is the first state in the country to pass the Right to Health Act, which provides a legal right to health.
D.The main objective of 'Heal in Rajasthan Policy' under Developed Rajasthan @2047 is to make the state a medical tourism hub.
(a)Only A, B and C✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)All A, B, C and D✓
(d)Only A and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The Government of Rajasthan's 'Vikas Rajasthan @2047' health vision aims to develop a universal, accessible and integrated health system in the state, encompassing physical, mental, social as well as spiritual well-being. Along with this, 'Heal in Rajasthan Policy' has been implemented to establish the state as a medical tourism center at the global level. Hence, all the four given statements are completely true.

Under the National Tuberculosis Elimination Program (NTEP), financial assistance of ₹ 500 per month is sent directly into the bank accounts (DBT) of TB patients under 'Nikshay Poshan Yojana' to ensure that they receive high quality nutritious food during the entire treatment period. Apart from this, through 'Nikshay Mitra' initiative, public representatives, Bhamashah and voluntary organizations adopt patients and provide them dry ration kits and nutritional support, due to which rapid progress is being made towards TB eradication in the state.
