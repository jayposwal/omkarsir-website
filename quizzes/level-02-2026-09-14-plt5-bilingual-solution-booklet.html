Q.1कोटा एवं हाड़ौती अंचल के प्राचीन मंदिरों के संदर्भ में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:

व्याख्या
हाड़ौती अंचल गुप्तकालीन और उत्तर-गुप्तकालीन मंदिर स्थापत्य की दृष्टि से अत्यंत समृद्ध रहा है। कोटा जिले का 'चारचौमा मंदिर' राजस्थान का प्राचीनतम गुप्तकालीन शिव मंदिर माना जाता है जहाँ गर्भगृह में पाँचवीं शताब्दी की काले पाषाण की चतुर्मुखी शिवलिंग प्रतिमा स्थापित है। कोटा का कंसुआ शिव मंदिर अत्यंत ऐतिहासिक है जहाँ 738 ईस्वी का एक महत्वपूर्ण शिलालेख प्राप्त हुआ है जो राजस्थान में अंतिम मौर्य राजा धवल की उपस्थिति का साक्ष्य देता है। मुकुंदरा की सुरम्य वादियों में स्थित दर्रा का गुप्त मंदिर भी इसी कालखंड की उत्कृष्ट पंचायतन वास्तुकला को प्रदर्शित करता है। अतः यहाँ दिए गए चारों कथन A, B, C और D पूरी तरह से सत्य हैं।
Q.1Consider the following statements with reference to the ancient temples of Kota and Hadoti region:
A.'Charchauma Shiva Temple' located in Charchauma village of Kota district is one of the oldest Gupta period temples of Rajasthan.
B.The Shiva temple of Sage Kanva located at Kansua (Kansua) in Kota is famous for the inscription of the Maurya king Dhaval of 738 A
C.The Shiva temple located at Darra (Mukundara Hills, Kota) is a prime example of the Panchayat style of the Gupta period.
D.The four-faced statue of Lord Shiva is installed in the Charchauma temple, which is an excellent example of sculpture of the Gupta period. Which of the above statements are true?
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Hadoti region has been extremely rich in terms of temple architecture of the Gupta period and post-Gupta period. 'Charchauma Temple' of Kota district is considered to be the oldest Gupta period Shiva temple of Rajasthan, where a fifth century black stone Chaturmukhi Shivalinga statue is installed in the sanctum sanctorum. The Kansua Shiva temple of Kota is extremely historical where an important inscription of 738 AD has been found which gives evidence of the presence of Dhaval, the last Maurya king in Rajasthan. The Gupta temple of Darra situated in the picturesque valleys of Mukundara also displays the excellent Panchayat architecture of this period. Hence, the four statements given here A, B, C and D are completely true.

Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to 'Harshnath Temple' located in Sikar district:
A.Harshnath Temple is an ancient Mahamaru style temple dedicated to Lord Shiva situated on Harsh Hill (Sikar).
B.The original construction of this grand temple was done by Chauhan king Guvak I of Shakambhari in the 10th century. The Harshnath inscription of
C.973 AD gives authentic information about the genealogy of the Chauhan dynasty and the then social system.
D.The main statue of this temple was destroyed by the army of Mughal ruler Aurangzeb. Which of the above statements are true?
(a)Only A, B and C✗
(b)Only B and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The temple of Harshnath situated on the 3000 feet high Harsh hill near Sikar is the main abode of Lord Shiva (Harshdev), the family deity of Chauhans. The construction of this temple was started by the Chauhan ruler Guvak I of Shakambhari and later it was expanded during the reign of Vigraharaja II. The Harsha inscription of 973 AD obtained from here is the strongest evidence to understand the early genealogy and administrative system of Chauhans. In 1679 AD, Aurangzeb's generals Bahadur Khan and Ruhullah Khan attacked this temple and caused heavy damage to its amazing carved sculptures, the remains of which are still visible in the temple premises. Hence all the statements are true.

Q.3Which statement is not correct with reference to the 'Shiv Temple of Badoli' (Ghateshwar Temple) situated on the banks of Chambal and Bamni rivers in Chittorgarh district?
(a)The Badoli temple complex is believed to have been built by Mihirkul, son of the Hun ruler Torman in the 9th-10th century.✗
(b)The most prominent and grand temple here is the Ghateshwar Mahadev Temple, in which the Nataraja form of Lord Shiva is very artistically carved.✗
(c)The credit for first bringing these historical temples to light goes to the famous historian Colonel James Todd in 1821 AD.✗
(d)The Badoli temple complex is the only Gupta period temple complex in Rajasthan built entirely of bricks.✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Option D is false because the Shiva temples of Badoli were not built with bricks but by skillfully joining red sandstone without lime (using dry stone technique). The Gupta period temple built with bricks is in Bhitargaon, Uttar Pradesh. There is a group of 8-9 temples in Badoli (Rawatbhata, Chittorgarh) in which the Ghateshwar Shiva temple is the most ornate. On the pillars of its theater, beautiful tunes and wonderful postures of Lord Shiva's Nataraja form are carved. Colonel James Todd, during his visit in 1821 AD, saw the artistry of these neglected temples and presented a detailed description of them to the western world.

Q.4Consider the following statements with reference to 'Shri Jagat Shiromani Mandir' located at Amer (Jaipur):
A.This temple was built by Maharaja Man Singh I's queen Kankawati in the memory of her late eldest son Jagat Singh.
B.In the sanctum sanctorum of this temple, the same historical black stone statue of Lord Krishna is installed which was worshiped by Meerambai in Chittorgarh.
C.A grand and artistic 'Garuda Mandap' made of white marble and black stone is situated in front of the temple.
D.This temple is built in Panchayat style and is also locally called 'Meeran Temple'. Which of the above statements are correct?
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Jagat Shiromani Temple situated at the foothills of Amer Fort is a unique confluence of Kachwaha architecture and Vaishnav devotion. Queen Kanakavati built it between 1599 and 1608 AD for the spiritual peace of her beloved Prince Jagat Singh after his untimely death. During the first Chittor campaign, Maharaja Mansingh brought the miraculous black stone idol of Girdhar Gopal worshiped by Mirabai from there and got it consecrated. The spire of the temple, the archway and the pavilion of Garudaji present a unique blend of South Indian and North Indian Nagara styles. Hence all statements A, B, C and D are completely correct.

Q.75राजस्थान के अमर शहीदों एवं क्रांतिकारियों के संदर्भ में निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:
A.जोधपुर के अमर सेनानी बालमुकुंद बिस्सा ने 1942 के भारत छोड़ो आंदोलन के दौरान जोधपुर सेंट्रल जेल में बदइंतजामी के विरुद्ध 36 दिन की भूख हड़ताल की और 19 जून 1942 को शहीद हो गए (इन्हें 'राजस्थान का जतिन दास' कहा जाता है)।

C.ठाकुर जोरावर सिंह बारहठ ने 23 दिसंबर 1912 को दिल्ली के चांदनी चौक में वायसराय लॉर्ड हार्डिंग के जुलूस पर बम फेंका था और आजीवन भूमिगत रहते हुए 'अमरदास बैरागी' के नाम से जंगलों में रहे।

व्याख्या
राजस्थान की धरती आजादी के परवानों के अद्वितीय बलिदान की साक्षी है। बालमुकुंद बिस्सा ने प्रजामंडल आंदोलन में चरखा संघ और मारवाड़ लोक परिषद के माध्यम से जनचेतना जगाई और जेल में भूख हड़ताल से वीरगति पाई। सागरमल गोपा की शहादत ने जैसलमेर रियासत की निरंकुशता को हिला दिया था जिसकी जांच हेतु गोपाल स्वरूप पाठक आयोग गठित हुआ था। जोरावर सिंह बारहठ ब्रिटिश पुलिस के लिए आजीवन पहेली बने रहे और कभी जीते जी पकड़े नहीं गए। डूंगजी-जवाहरजी ने 1847 में नसीराबाद छावनी को लूटकर अंग्रेजों को खुली चुनौती दी थी। अतः सभी चारों कथन A, B, C और D पूर्णतः सत्य हैं।
Q.75Consider the following statements with reference to the immortal martyrs and revolutionaries of Rajasthan:
A.Balmukund Bissa, the immortal fighter of Jodhpur, went on a 36-day hunger strike against the mismanagement in Jodhpur Central Jail during the Quit India Movement of 1942 and was martyred on 19 June 1942 (he is called 'Jatin Das of Rajasthan').
B.Jaisalmer's great revolutionary Sagarmal Gopa exposed the royal atrocities by writing the books 'Jaisalmer Ka Gundaraj' and 'Raghunath Singh Ka Sude', due to which he was burnt alive by pouring kerosene in the jail on 3 April 1946.
C.Thakur Zorawar Singh Barhath threw a bomb at the procession of Viceroy Lord Hardinge in Chandni Chowk, Delhi on 23 December 1912 and lived underground for the rest of his life in the forests under the name of 'Amardas Bairagi'.
D.Dungji and Jawaharji (Kachwaha Rajputs of Bathoth-Patoda of Sikar) were worshiped as folk deities who looted Nasirabad cantonment before 1857 and distributed the wealth among the poor. Which of the above statements are true?
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
The land of Rajasthan is witness to the unique sacrifice of the freedom fighters. Balmukund Bissa raised public awareness in Prajamandal movement through Charkha Sangh and Marwar Lok Parishad and attained martyrdom through hunger strike in jail. The martyrdom of Sagarmal Gopa had shaken the autocracy of the Jaisalmer state, for which Gopal Swaroop Pathak Commission was formed to investigate. Zorawar Singh Barhath remained a lifelong enigma for the British police and was never caught alive. Dungji-Jawaharji had openly challenged the British by looting Nasirabad cantonment in 1847. Hence, all the four statements A, B, C and D are completely true.
