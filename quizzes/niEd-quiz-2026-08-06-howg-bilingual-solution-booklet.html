व्याख्या
राजस्थान में कृषि गणना प्रत्येक 5 वर्ष के अंतराल पर कृषि एवं सहकारिता विभाग द्वारा आयोजित की जाती है। इस गणना की संदर्भ अवधि जुलाई से जून तक (कृषि वर्ष) होती है, जिसके अंतर्गत राज्य में कृषि जोतों के आकार, भूमि उपयोग और सिंचाई के साधनों का व्यापक विश्लेषण किया जाता है। नवीनतम आधिकारिक कृषि गणना के आंकड़े वर्ष 2015-16 के अनुसार क्रियान्वित हैं। विकल्प A गलत है क्योंकि 10 वर्षों में जनसांख्यिकी जनगणना होती है, कृषि गणना नहीं। विकल्प C और D भी गलत हैं क्योंकि 3 वर्ष या 1 वर्ष का कोई प्रावधान नहीं है।
Q.1Agricultural census is conducted in Rajasthan at an interval of how many years and by which department?
(a)Every 10 years – by Revenue Department✗
(b)Every 5 years - by the Department of Agriculture and Cooperation✓
(c)Every 3 years – by the Statistics Department✗
(d)Every 1 year - by Rural Development Department✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Agricultural census in Rajasthan is conducted by the Department of Agriculture and Cooperation at an interval of every 5 years. The reference period of this calculation is from July to June (agricultural year), under which a comprehensive analysis of the size of agricultural holdings, land use and means of irrigation in the state is done. The latest official agriculture census data is as per the year 2015-16. Option A is incorrect because Demographic Census is conducted every 10 years and not Agricultural Census. Options C and D are also incorrect as there is no provision for 3 years or 1 year.

(b)Only B, C and D✗
(c)Only A, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✓

All the four given statements reflect the actual situation of agriculture in Rajasthan. Q.3Consider the following statements regarding the recent Index of Agricultural Production and crop season in Rajasthan:
A.The base year of the recent Index of Agricultural Production in Rajasthan has been considered to be 2005-06 to 2007-08.
B.The agricultural year in Rajasthan is considered to start from 1st July to 30th June.
C.There are mainly three crop seasons in the state – Rabi, Kharif and Zaid.
D.The main base year of Agricultural Production Index has been made permanent from 1991-92 to 1993-94.

(b)Only B and C✗
(c)Only A and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗

Statements A, B and C are completely true. Q.7What is the time period of 'Zaid' crop and its main yield in Rajasthan?

(b)March to June – Watermelon, melon and vegetables✓
(c)January to April – Wheat and gram✗

Option A is Kharif harvesting period, Option C is Rabi harvesting period and Option D is latter part of Kharif.

Q.70Consider the following statements regarding Agro-Climatic Zones of Rajasthan:
A.Rajasthan has been divided into a total of 10 Agro-Climatic Zones by the Agriculture Department.
B.The largest agro-climatic region in terms of area is 'I-C' (extremely dry partially irrigated region), while the smallest is 'IV-B' (humid southern plain).
C.Out of the 15 agro-climatic regions of India, Rajasthan mainly consists of 2 regions (8th and 14th).
D.The average rainfall in all the 10 agro-climatic zones of Rajasthan is equally 100 cm.
(a)Only A, B and C✓
(b)Only B and D✗
(c)Only C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Statements A, B and C are completely correct. The state has 10 agroclimatic divisions, with I-C being the largest and IV-B being the smallest. Out of the 15 climatic zones of India, Rajasthan has 2 (8th Malwa Plateau and 14th Western Dry Zone). Statement D is incorrect because rainfall varies by 10-30 cm in the western dry regions and by 70-100 cm in the south-eastern humid regions.
