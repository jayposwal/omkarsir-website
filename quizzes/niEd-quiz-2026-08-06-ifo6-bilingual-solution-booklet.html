Q.1आर्थिक समीक्षा 2025-26 के अनुसार राजस्थान की कृषि एवं संबद्ध क्षेत्र की अर्थव्यवस्था से संबंधित निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:

B.In terms of area, the largest agro-climatic region of Rajasthan is Zone IC (very dry partially irrigated).

(b)Only A and C✗
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗

Explanation

Hence statement C is wrong because zone IB is not the smallest but the second smallest zone.

The biggest strength of Rajasthan's agriculture is its livestock sector (49.35% GVA share) and diversification of crops in which the state ranks first in the country in guar (88.8%), mustard (43.4%) and millet (41.3%). With digital initiatives like Raj-AIIMS, Rajkisan Sathi and drone technology and farming suited to the agro-climatic regions (10 zones), the state is continuously moving on the path of progress.
