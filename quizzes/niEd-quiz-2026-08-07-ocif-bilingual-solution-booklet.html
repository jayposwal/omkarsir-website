(c)70.9 वर्ष✗

(e)अनुत्तरित प्रश्न✗
व्याख्या
राजस्थान में वर्ष 2013-17 की संदर्भ अवधि में जन्म के समय पुरुष जीवन प्रत्याशा 66.3 वर्ष दर्ज की गई थी, जबकि इसी अवधि में भारत की औसत पुरुष जीवन प्रत्याशा अधिक थी। नवीनतम नमूना पंजीयन प्रणाली (SRS) 2016-20 रिपोर्ट के अनुसार राजस्थान की कुल औसत जीवन प्रत्याशा दर बढ़कर 69.4 वर्ष हो गई है, जबकि संपूर्ण भारत की औसत जीवन प्रत्याशा 70.0 वर्ष दर्ज की गई है। जीवन प्रत्याशा दर किसी क्षेत्र में स्वास्थ्य सेवाओं की गुणवत्ता, शिशु मृत्यु दर एवं पोषण स्तर का प्रमुख सूचकांक होती है। विकल्प A (66.8 वर्ष) महिला जीवन प्रत्याशा से निकटता दर्शाता है, विकल्प C (70.9 वर्ष) राष्ट्रीय स्तर के उच्च संकेतकों को दर्शाता है तथा विकल्प D (68.5 वर्ष) पूर्ववर्ती अनुमानों का भाग रहा है, अतः सही उत्तर विकल्प B है।
Q.1What was the male life expectancy at birth recorded in Rajasthan during the period 2013-17?
(a)66.8 years✗
(b)66.3 years✓
(c)70.9 years✗
(d)68.5 years✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
Male life expectancy at birth in Rajasthan was recorded at 66.3 years in the reference period 2013–17, while India's average male life expectancy in the same period was higher. According to the latest Sample Registration System (SRS) 2016-20 report, the overall average life expectancy rate of Rajasthan has increased to 69.4 years, while the average life expectancy of all India has been recorded at 70.0 years. Life expectancy rate is a major index of the quality of health services, infant mortality rate and nutrition level in an area. Option A (66.8 years) reflects closeness to female life expectancy, option C (70.9 years) reflects high national level indicators and option D (68.5 years) has been part of earlier estimates, hence the correct answer is option B.

Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to the demographic data of Rajasthan as per Census 2011:
A.The sex ratio of children in the age group of 0-6 years in the state is 888.
B.The total percentage of female literacy in the state has been recorded at 52.1%.
C.The percentage of Scheduled Tribes (ST) in the total population of the state is 13.5%.
D.The percentage of urban population in Rajasthan is 24.9% and rural population is 75.1%.
(a)Only A and B are correct✗
(b)Only B and C are correct✗
(c)Only A, B and D are correct✗
(d)A, B, C and D are all true✓
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
According to Census 2011, all the demographic indices of Rajasthan are completely true in the given statements. Scheduled Tribe (ST) population is 13.5 percent of the total population (while SC population is 17.83 percent). Additionally, 24.9 percent of the state's population resides in cities while 75.1 percent of the population lives in rural areas. Since all four statements are factually correct, option D (all are true) is the correct answer.

(c)Vulnerable population in emergency✓

World Population Day is celebrated every year on 11 July and its official theme for the year 2015 was 'Vulnerable Populations in Emergencies'. Q.5In the span of 60 years from 1951 to 2011, a historical increase of more than how many times has been recorded in the total literacy rate of Rajasthan?
(a)5 times✗
(b)6 times✗
(c)8 times✓
(d)10 times✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
In the first census of 1951, the total literacy rate of Rajasthan was only 8.50% (male literacy 13.88% and female literacy 2.66%). By the 2011 census, literacy in the state had increased to 66.11%, marking a historic increase of more than 8 times (approximately 7.78 to 8 times) compared to 1951. During this period, an unprecedented increase of about 19.5 times has been recorded in female literacy from 2.66% to 52.1%. Options A, B and D do not match the actual mathematical ratio of growth, hence option C is the correct answer.

Q.27Consider the statements regarding the schemes related to Mid-Day Meal and PM Nutrition in Rajasthan:

D.'Shri Krishna Bhog Yojana' has been notified for feeding children or donating utensils/gas stove on festivals/birthdays from March 1, 2024.

(b)Only B and D✗

(d)All A, B, C and D✓

Explanation
All four statements related to school nutrition and mid-day meal system are completely factual and true.
