Q.1Consider the following statements with reference to Rajasthan Skill Policy-2025:
A.Under this, administrative approval has been given for the implementation of Recruit-Train-Deploy (RTD) model.
B.This decision was taken in the 50th board meeting of Rajasthan Skill and Livelihood Development Corporation (RSLDC) on 22 June 2026.
C.Under this policy, students studying regularly in school or college will also be eligible for training.
D.The training program will include a 120-hour mandatory employment-readiness module and on-the-job training (OJT).
(a)Only A, B and C✗
(b)Only A, B and D✓
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗

Explanation
Administrative approval was given in the 50th board meeting of RSLDC (22 June 2026) for the implementation of Recruit-Train-Deploy (RTD) model under Rajasthan Skill Policy-2025. Only unemployed youth aged 18 years and above are eligible in this scheme, whereas students studying regularly in school or college cannot participate in it, hence statement C is incorrect. Training includes a 120-hour mandatory employment-readiness module and on-the-job training (OJT). On completion of the training, third party evaluation will be conducted and training partner agencies will be selected through Expression of Interest (EoI). Hence the correct answer is option B.

Q.2Consider the following statements with reference to the draft provisions of 'Rajasthan Energy Conservation and Sustainable Building Code, 2026':
A.This code will be applicable to buildings whose built-up area is 2000 square meters or more.
B.Buildings with 100 kW or more connected load and 120 KVA or more connected demand will come under its purview.
C.Buildings meeting the standards of Super Building Code will be given 15 percent additional built area ratio (BAR).
D.4% of the contracted power demand in buildings or 50% of the available roof top area will have to be reserved for renewable energy.
(a)Only A and B✗
(b)Only A, B and D✓
(c)Only B, C and D✗
(d)All A, B, C and D✗
(e)Question not attempted✗
Explanation
This code, being prepared by Rajasthan Renewable Energy Corporation, includes buildings with 2000 square meter or more built-up area, 100 kW connected load and 120 KVA connected demand. Under Green Building, there is a provision of additional Built Area Ratio (BAR) of 5% if the plus standards of the code are followed and 10% if the Super Building Code standards are met (not 15%), hence statement C is false. Apart from this, it has been made mandatory to reserve 4% of the contracted power demand or 50% of the available rooftop area for renewable energy. An integrated web portal will also be developed for its effective implementation. Hence option B is correct.

Explanation
