11. Précis Writing क्या है? — मूल सिद्धांत

🔑 Précis (प्रेसी)

तकनीकी अर्थ: यह एक फ्रेंच शब्द है जिसका अर्थ है — जो कुछ भी अनावश्यक (inessential) है उसे हटा देना। Précis किसी लेख, भाषण या गद्यांश का छोटा रूप है, जिसमें सिर्फ मुख्य बिंदु/विचार होते हैं, बिना विस्तृत वर्णन के।
आसान भाषा में: जैसे किसी 10 मिनट के भाषण को किसी को 2 मिनट में बताना हो, तो आप सिर्फ मुख्य बातें बताएंगे, हर उदाहरण या कहानी नहीं दोहराएंगे — Précis भी वैसे ही मूल गद्यांश की सिर्फ आत्मा (essence) पकड़ती है।
उदाहरण: RPSC के अनुसार Précis को 'Summary Writing' भी कहा जा सकता है — दोनों नाम एक ही चीज़ के लिए प्रयोग होते हैं।

⚠️ ध्यान दें
⚠️ मूल सिद्धांत (Core Principle): Précis सामान्यतः मूल गद्यांश की लंबाई का लगभग 1/3 भाग होती है — इसलिए सटीक शब्द-गिनती को लेकर ज़्यादा तनाव लेने की ज़रूरत नहीं। Précis प्रशासनिक उपयोग (strict administrative purpose) के लिए होती है, इसलिए इसमें कोई सजावट (Idioms/Phrases/Beautification), भूमिका (introduction) या Quotation नहीं होनी चाहिए — सीधी, सरल, तथ्यात्मक भाषा में लिखें।

22. Précis का प्रारूप (Format)

परीक्षा में Précis हमेशा इसी प्रारूप में लिखी जाती है — Title पहले या बाद में लिखें (नीचे नियम 9-10 देखें), पर अंतिम उत्तर में Title ऊपर और Précis नीचे, एक ही Paragraph में, दोनों तरफ ठीक से Align होकर लिखी जानी चाहिए।

✏️ Précis का प्रारूप — जैसा उत्तर-पुस्तिका में लिखना है

उत्तर :- Précis WritingTitle :- ..............................Précis :-.............................................................................................................................................................................................................................. [Words :- 60]

⚠️ ध्यान दें
⚠️ ध्यान रखें: पूरी Précis एक ही Paragraph में लिखें — उसे कई छोटे Paragraphs में मत बांटिए। Title के बाद Full Stop नहीं लगाना है।

33. Title लिखने के 14 नियम

  • 1. Title के बाद Full Stop नहीं लगाना है।
  • 2. Title 3-4 शब्दों से ज़्यादा लंबा नहीं होना चाहिए।
  • 3. Title एक पूरा Sentence नहीं होना चाहिए — जहाँ तक संभव हो Title में Verb/Helping Verb न लिखें।
  • 4. पहले पूरा गद्यांश पढ़कर उसका केंद्रीय भाव (Central Theme/Idea) समझें — Title उसी पर आधारित होना चाहिए।
  • 5. प्रश्न में Title मांगा जाए या न मांगा जाए, आपको हमेशा लिखना ज़रूरी है।
  • 6. Title छोटा और प्रभावशाली (Short and Sweet) होना चाहिए।
  • 7. Title में कभी कोई Verb नहीं होना चाहिए।
  • 8. Title के सभी शब्द Capital Letter से शुरू होने चाहिए।
  • 9. Title की शुरुआत में मत लिखिए — पहले 'Title :- ...........' जगह छोड़ दीजिए।
  • 10. पहले पूरी Précis लिख लीजिए, उसके बाद Title लिखिए — इससे पूरा भाव समझकर बेहतर Title बना पाएंगे।
  • 11. जहाँ तक संभव हो, Title में Article (a, an, the) का प्रयोग न करें।
  • 12. Title में ' - ' या ' : ' या ' , ' जैसे विराम-चिह्नों का प्रयोग किया जा सकता है (जैसे 'Books : True Friends')।

गलत (पूरा Sentence) बनाम सही (छोटा Title) — उदाहरण

✅ सही (यह एक Title है)❌ गलत (यह एक Sentence है)
✔ Importance of Health✘ Health is wealth.
✔ Role of Education✘ Education is important.
✔ Power of Knowledge✘ Knowledge is power.
✔ Value / Importance of Work✘ Work is worship.
✔ Power of Art✘ Art is power.

Step 1: पहला वाचन — केंद्रीय भाव पकड़ें

गद्यांश को ध्यान से पढ़ें और समझने की कोशिश करें कि लेखक मूल रूप से क्या कहना चाह रहा है (Central Theme/Idea)। Précis और Title, दोनों इसी केंद्रीय भाव पर आधारित होने चाहिए।

Step 2: दूसरा वाचन — Underline करें (यही असली Secret है!)

गद्यांश को दोबारा पढ़ें। जो लाइनें समझ में नहीं आ रहीं, उन्हें फ़िलहाल छोड़ दें। जो लाइनें अच्छी तरह समझ आ रही हैं, उन्हें Underline कर लें — बस यही है Précis लिखने का पूरा राज़।

Step 3: तीसरा वाचन — सिर्फ Underline की गई लाइनें पढ़ें

अब सिर्फ Underline किए गए हिस्सों को पढ़ें और यह समझने की कोशिश करें कि वे मिलकर क्या बात कह रहे हैं। आमतौर पर ऐसी 4-5 पंक्तियाँ ज़रूर मिल जाएंगी जो पूरी तरह समझ आ रही हों — बस यहीं से आपका Confidence बनेगा। जो थोड़ी-बहुत लाइनें समझ न आएं, उन्हें अपनी समझ के अनुसार हल्के में लपेटकर (paraphrase करके) लिख दें — कोई बात नहीं।

Step 4: अपने शब्दों में लिखें (Own Words)

Underline किए गए भाग को अपने शब्दों में लिखने की कोशिश करें। RBSE/CBSE का नियम है — भाषा सरल, व्याकरण की दृष्टि से शुद्ध, स्पष्ट, संक्षिप्त होनी चाहिए, परंतु सभी ज़रूरी बिंदु (essential points) शामिल होने चाहिए।

⚠️ ध्यान दें
⚠️ शुरुआत कैसे करें?: Précis की शुरुआत हमेशा Noun (संज्ञा) से करें — Pronoun (He/She/I/We/It/They) से शुरुआत न करें। बाद के वाक्यों में उसी Noun के लिए Pronoun का प्रयोग किया जा सकता है — बल्कि जितना हो सके, आगे Pronoun का ही अधिक प्रयोग करें। Capital Letter का प्रयोग तीन जगह होता है — (क) Proper Noun में, (ख) Sentence की शुरुआत में, (ग) Full Stop के बाद।
⚠️ ध्यान दें
⚠️ सावधानी — Word Limit का ध्यान रखें: शुरुआत में ही 2-4 पंक्तियों में 50% Précis मत लिख डालिए — बाद में शब्द ही खत्म हो जाएंगे जबकि बात अभी बाकी होगी। पहले Underline करके अंदाज़ा लगाइए कि हर हिस्से को कितने शब्दों में समेटना है, फिर तदनुसार लिखिए।

55. क्या न लिखें — 19 महत्वपूर्ण बातें

तथ्यों से जुड़े नियम

  • ➤ Paragraph में न दिया गया कोई तथ्य (fact) अपनी तरफ़ से मत लिखिए — अपना ज्ञान मत जोड़िए।
  • ➤ गद्यांश में दिए गए तथ्यों की सीमा से कभी बाहर मत जाइए। अपनी तरफ़ से कोई निष्कर्ष (conclusion) मत निकालिए।
  • ➤ गद्यांश की किसी भी गलत/सही जानकारी को अपनी तरफ़ से मत बदलिए।
  • ➤ अपने विचार (own ideas) मत लिखिए — सिर्फ गद्यांश में जो लिखा है, वही अपने शब्दों में लिखें।

भाषा-शैली से जुड़े नियम — इनका प्रयोग न करें

प्रयोग न करेंउदाहरण
Idioms, Phrases और परिभाषाएं (Definitions)किसी मुहावरे या शब्द की परिभाषा देना
Abbreviations और दोहराव (Repetition)एक ही बात को अलग-अलग तरीकों से दोबारा कहना
उदाहरण, वर्णन और स्पष्टीकरण (Examples/Illustrations)गद्यांश में दी गई कहानी/उदाहरण को दोबारा बताना
'I/We' प्रथम-पुरुष का अधिक प्रयोग'I think this passage says...' जैसे वाक्य
प्रश्न और उद्धरण चिह्न (Questions and Quotations)गद्यांश के किसी संवाद को ज्यों-का-त्यों उद्धृत करना
Direct Speechसंवादों को हमेशा Indirect Speech में बदलकर लिखें
Should/Must/Ought to/Suggestion वाले वाक्यजहाँ तक हो सके इनका प्रयोग थोड़ा कम करें

ये वाक्यांश (Phrases) कभी न लिखें

Précis में अपनी राय जताने वाले या गद्यांश का हवाला देने वाले वाक्यांश बिल्कुल न लिखें, जैसे:

"According to the author/writer/passage" • "The author/writer says that" • "It is clear that" • "It is said that / asked that" • "I think / I believe" • "I like / I want / I agree" • "According to my opinion"

⚠️ ध्यान दें
⚠️ एक और ज़रूरी बात: Précis को अलग-अलग Paragraphs में मत बांटिए — पूरी Précis एक ही Paragraph में लिखी जानी चाहिए। साथ ही, उत्तर-पुस्तिका में लिखावट (Handwriting) साफ़, खुली और बड़े अक्षरों में होनी चाहिए।

66. Précis को बेहतर व संक्षिप्त बनाने वाले Conjunctions

कई छोटे-छोटे वाक्यों को जोड़कर एक बेहतर, संक्षिप्त (terse) वाक्य बनाने के लिए इन Conjunctions का भरपूर प्रयोग करें — इससे Précis ज़्यादा प्रभावशाली और कम शब्दों में बनती है:

And • But • Who • Which • That • Not only ... but also • Both ... and • As well as • Because • Since • Although

77. 11 पूर्ण हल किए गए उदाहरण (Passage → Title → Précis)

नीचे वही 11 गद्यांश दिए गए हैं जो पिछले अध्याय (Unseen Passage) में पढ़े थे — अब इन्हीं पर Précis लिखने की तकनीक लागू करके देखें। पहले खुद गद्यांश पढ़कर अपनी Précis लिखने की कोशिश करें, फिर नीचे मॉडल उत्तर से तुलना करें।

उदाहरण 1

📖 Passage 1 — मूल गद्यांश (लगभग 254 शब्द)

In everyday speech, we talk of having a good memory, of having a poor memory, of having a better memory for faces than for names, of having a memory that is failing, and so on. Such talk suggests that memory is an object, a thing which we possess in the same way as we possess a head or a big toe. Yet it is true, although alarming, to say that there is no such thing as memory. A big toe can be seen and touched, but not so memory. Should an acquaintance boast of having an excellent memory, we cannot confirm his claim in the same way as if he boasted the possession of a well- stocked. library. We cannot ask him to show us his memory. What we could do, how-ever, is to let him read the page of a book, then have him close the book and try to recite what he has just read. If he reels off the page verbatim, we conclude that his claim is, at the least, not without foundation. But we have not observed anything which could be called a memory. We have given him an opportunity to learn something and then to demonstrate how well he remembers it. We have not examined anything which he has, but have watched him doing something, namely, repeating or trying to repeat something he has read. In short, we have concerned ourselves, not with an object but with an activity, not with his memory but with his activity of remembering.

✅ मॉडल Précis

Title :- Memory as an Activity, Not a Thing
Précis :-
People often speak of memory as if it were a thing we possess, like a body part. However, memory cannot be seen or touched. We cannot directly prove someone has a good memory by looking at it. Instead, we test it by observing how well a person remembers something, such as repeating a passage after reading it. This shows that memory is not an object but an activity of remembering.
[Words :- 70]

उदाहरण 2

📖 Passage 2 — मूल गद्यांश (लगभग 222 शब्द)

The thoughtless, the ignorant and the indolent seeing only the apparent effect of things and not the things themselves talk of luck of fortunes and chance. Seeing a man grow rich they say "How lucky he is!" Observing another becomes intellectual they exclaim, "How highly favoured he is!" And noting the saintly character and wide influence of someone else they remark, "How chance aids him at every turn!" They do not see the trials and failures and struggles which these men have voluntarily encountered in order to gain their experience, have no knowledge of the sacrifices they have made, of the undaunted efforts they have put forth, of the faith they have exercised that they might overcome the apparently insurmountable and realise the vision of their heart. They do not know the darkness and the heartaches; they only see the light and joy; do not see the long and arduous journey but only behold the pleasant goal and call it "good fortune; do not understand the process but only perceive the results and call it "chance". In all human affairs there are efforts and there are results and the strength of effort is the measure of the result. Chance is not. Gifts, powers, material, intellectual and spiritual possessions are the fruits of effort - they are thoughts completed, objects accomplished, visions realized.

✅ मॉडल Précis

Title :- Success the Result of Effort, Not Luck
Précis :-
People often think success comes from luck or chance because they only see the results, not the effort behind them. When someone becomes rich, intelligent, or respected, they call it good fortune. However, they do not see the hard work, struggles, sacrifices, and determination that led to that success. In reality, success is the result of continuous effort, not chance. All achievements, whether material or intellectual, are gained through hard work and strong effort.
[Words :- 74]

उदाहरण 3

📖 Passage 3 — मूल गद्यांश (लगभग 259 शब्द)

Conversation is indeed the most easily teachable of all arts. All you need to do in order to become a good conversationalist is to find a subject that interests you and your listeners. There are, for example, numberless hobbies to talk about. But the important thing is that you must talk about the other fellow's hobby rather than your own. Therein lies the secret of your popularity. Talk to your friends about things that interest them and you will make your reputation for good fellowship, charming wit and a brilliant mind. There is nothing that pleases people more than your interest in their interest. It is as important to know what subject to avoid as what subjects to select for good conversation. If you don't want to be set down as a wet blanket or a bore, be careful to avoid unpleasant topics. Avoid talking about yourself, unless you are asked to do so. People are interested in their problems not in yours. Sickness and death bore everybody. The only one who willingly listens to such talk is a doctor, but he gets paid for it. To be a good conversationalist you must know not only what to say but how to say it. Be civil and modest. Don't ever emphasize your own happiness. Be mentally quick and witty, but don't hurt others with your wit. Finally try to avoid mannerisms in your conversation. Don't bite your lips, or click your tongue, or roll your eyes, or use hands excessively as you speak. Such gestures detract from the conversation.

📖 Passage 5 — मूल गद्यांश (लगभग 287 शब्द)

Sleep is essential for a person's health and well-being. Yet millions of people do not get enough sleep and many suffer from lack of sleep. Most of those with these problems go undiagnosed and untreated. In addition, more than 40 per cent of adults experience daytime sleepiness severe enough to interfere with their daily activities at least a few days each month - with 20 per cent reporting problems of sleepiness a few days a week or more. Furthermore, 69 per cent of children experience one or more sleep problems a few nights or more during a week. If a sleep-deprived person doesn't sleep after the initial signs, the person may then start to experience apathy, slowed speech and flattened emotional responses, impaired memory and an inability to be novel or multitask. As a person gets to the point of falling asleep, he or she will fall into micro sleeps (5-10 seconds) that cause lapses in attention, nod off while doing an activity like driving or reading and then finally experience hallucinations. Everyone's individual sleep needs vary. In general, most healthy adults are built for 16 hours of wakefulness and need an average of eight hours of sleep a night. However, some individuals are able to function without sleepiness or drowsiness after as little as six hours of sleep. Others can't perform at their peak unless they've slept ten hours. And contrary to common myth, the need for sleep doesn't decline with age but the ability to sleep for six to eight hours at one time may be reduced. It is a good idea to talk to a physician, or mental health provider about any sleeping problem that recurs or persists for longer than a few weeks.

✅ मॉडल Précis

Title :- Importance of Proper Sleep
Précis :-
Sleep is very important for good health, yet many people do not get enough of it. Lack of sleep can cause problems like tiredness, poor memory, slow thinking, and even hallucinations. People need different amounts of sleep, but most adults require about eight hours daily. Sleep problems should not be ignored, and medical help should be taken if they continue for a long time.

📖 Passage 11 — मूल गद्यांश (लगभग 260 शब्द)

Rajasthan, one of the driest states of India, has for centuries taught the world a valuable lesson in water conservation. Long before pipelines and government schemes existed, the people of this arid land had developed remarkable methods of harvesting every drop of rain that fell upon its sandy soil. Structures such as johads, tankas, and baoris were built not by engineers with modern machines, but by ordinary villagers who understood that survival in the desert depended on storing water during the short monsoon for use through the long dry months. A tanka, for instance, is an underground tank built beneath a courtyard to collect rainwater from rooftops, keeping it clean and cool for months. A johad, on the other hand, is a small earthen check-dam that captures rainwater and allows it to seep slowly into the ground, recharging the water table for the entire village. These systems, developed through generations of trial and observation, were remarkably suited to the local climate and required no external energy to operate. In recent decades, as groundwater levels fell due to over-extraction and changing rainfall patterns, many villages returned to reviving these traditional structures instead of relying solely on borewells. Social organisations, working alongside local communities, have restored hundreds of johads and tankas, turning several dry villages green again and reversing large-scale migration in some regions. The revival of these age-old techniques proves an important truth: sustainable solutions to modern environmental problems often already exist in traditional wisdom; they only need to be recognised, respected, and restored rather than replaced entirely by imported technology.

✅ मॉडल Précis

Title :- Traditional Water Wisdom of Rajasthan
Précis :-
Rajasthan's traditional water-harvesting structures like tankas and johads, built by villagers over generations, efficiently stored and recharged water in this dry region. Tankas collected rooftop rainwater underground, while johads let rainwater seep into the ground and recharge the water table. As groundwater declined due to over-extraction and erratic rainfall, communities revived these old methods instead of depending only on borewells, restoring greenery and reducing migration. This revival shows that traditional wisdom often holds sustainable solutions to modern environmental problems, which merely need to be revived rather than replaced.
[Words :- 88]
